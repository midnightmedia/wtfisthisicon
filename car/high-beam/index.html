Car dashboard · Blue light

Full beam (high beam)

Also called: high beam, main beam, brights

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Your full-beam / high-beam headlights are on. Brilliant for you, blinding for anyone coming the other way.

What to do about it

Great on empty, dark roads. Switch back to dipped beam (usually by pulling or pushing the indicator stalk) when there's oncoming traffic or a car in front, or you'll dazzle them. They tend to let you know.
