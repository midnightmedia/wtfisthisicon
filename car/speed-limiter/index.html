Car dashboard · Green light

Speed limiter (LIM)

Also called: LIM, speed limit function, adjustable speed limiter, ASL, intelligent speed assistance, ISA

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The speed limiter is on. You set a top speed and the car won't go faster than that, however hard you press the accelerator. Handy for 20 mph zones and average speed cameras.

What to do about it

Set or adjust the speed with the cruise control buttons on the steering wheel (often a LIM button or switch picks the limiter instead of cruise). Need a burst of speed to overtake? On most cars, pushing the pedal right to the floor overrides it.

For the record

Many newer cars also read speed limit signs and beep when you go over (intelligent speed assistance). You can usually turn that off for the journey, but it switches itself back on next time.
