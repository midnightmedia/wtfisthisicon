Car dashboard · Green light

Sidelights / parking lights

Also called: position lights, parking lamps, side lights

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Your sidelights (the small parking/position lights) are on, but your headlights aren't.

What to do about it

Fine for parking or at dusk, but switch to proper headlights when it's dark. Sidelights alone aren't enough to see or be seen.
