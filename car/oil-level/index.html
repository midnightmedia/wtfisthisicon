Car dashboard · Amber light

Low engine oil level (oil can with a wavy line)

Also called: oil level low, oil level warning, MIN oil, top up oil

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The engine oil level is getting low. It's the calmer, amber cousin of the red oil pressure light: the engine is still being lubricated, but it wants a top-up before that changes.

What to do about it

Check the level soon: park on level ground, switch off, wait a few minutes, then use the dipstick (or the oil level menu, if your car has no dipstick). Top up with the oil grade in your handbook, a little at a time, and don't overfill. If it keeps dropping quickly, get it checked for leaks.

For the record

VW, Audi, Skoda and Seat use this can-with-a-wave symbol too. If the oil can has no wave and a drip from the spout, and it's red, stop and switch off: that's the oil pressure warning.
