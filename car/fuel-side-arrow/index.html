Car dashboard

Arrow next to the fuel pump

Also called: fuel door indicator, fuel filler side, petrol cap side, fuel tank side arrow

WTF it means

The little triangle next to the fuel pump symbol on the fuel gauge points to the side of the car the fuel cap is on. That's all it does, and it's genuinely useful.

What to do about it

Glance at it before you pull into the petrol station, especially in a hire car, and you'll get the right side of the pump first time.

For the record

The hose on the pump picture isn't a reliable clue: on plenty of cars it's on the wrong side. Trust the arrow.
