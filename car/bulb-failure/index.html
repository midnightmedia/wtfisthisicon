Car dashboard · Amber light

Bulb failure

Also called: lamp out, light failure, exterior light fault

Sort it soon

WTF it means

One of the exterior lights (headlight, brake light, indicator, number plate light) has blown. The car knows which one. It just isn't saying.

What to do about it

Walk round the car with the lights on (and get someone to press the brake) to find the dead bulb, then replace it. Driving with a blown light can get you fined.
