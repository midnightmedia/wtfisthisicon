Car dashboard · Green light

Automatic high beam (A in a headlight)

Also called: auto high beam, AHB, high beam assist, auto main beam, smart beam

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Automatic high beam is switched on. A camera behind the windscreen switches between full beam and dipped headlights for you, dipping when it sees other cars' lights or street lights.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. If it's dazzling people or dipping too late, switch it off and use the stalk yourself. A dirty or misted windscreen in front of the mirror confuses the camera.

For the record

With slanted lines instead of straight ones, an A in a headlight usually means automatic headlights instead.
