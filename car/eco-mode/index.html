Car dashboard · Green light

Eco mode

Also called: ECO, economy mode, eco drive

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Eco driving mode is on. To save fuel, the car softens the accelerator, changes gear earlier and dials back the air-con. Hence the leaf.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. If you want more response, switch it off with the drive-mode button.
