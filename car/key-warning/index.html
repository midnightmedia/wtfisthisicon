Car dashboard · Amber light

Key not detected / immobiliser

Also called: immobiliser, smart key not found, key fob battery low, security light

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The car can't detect your key, often because the fob battery is dying or the key is out of range. If it flashes while the car is parked and locked, that's just the security system showing it's armed.

What to do about it

Hold the key fob right against the start button (or steering column), as if introducing them. Most cars can read it there even with a dead battery. Replace the fob battery soon.
