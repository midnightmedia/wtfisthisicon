Car dashboard · Green light

Dipped headlights (low beam)

Also called: low beam, dipped beam, headlights on

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Your normal headlights are on. Comfortably the least alarming jellyfish on the dashboard.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. This is the setting for night driving and bad weather.
