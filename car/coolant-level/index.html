Car dashboard · Amber light

Low coolant level

Also called: coolant warning, antifreeze low, radiator fluid low, expansion tank low

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The coolant (antifreeze) in the expansion tank has dropped below the minimum mark. Coolant stops the engine overheating, so this is the early warning before the temperature light comes on.

What to do about it

When the engine is cold, open the bonnet, find the expansion tank (usually a see-through plastic bottle with MIN and MAX marks) and top up with the coolant type in your handbook. Never open the cap while the engine is hot. If it needs topping up again soon, there's a leak: get it checked.

For the record

Some cars use the thermometer-in-waves symbol for low coolant too, so read any message on the display. If the light turns red or the temperature gauge climbs, pull over and switch off.
