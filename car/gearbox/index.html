Car dashboard · Amber light

Gearbox / transmission warning

Also called: transmission temperature, automatic transmission fault, AT warning

Sort it soon

WTF it means

There's a problem with the gearbox (usually an automatic). Often it's overheating, for example from towing or crawling along in heavy traffic. Nobody enjoys a traffic jam, gearboxes included.

What to do about it

If it's overheating, pull over and let it cool down with the engine idling in Park. If the light comes back or stays on, get the transmission checked soon.
