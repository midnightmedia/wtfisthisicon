Car dashboard · Red light

Engine temperature warning

Also called: coolant temperature, engine overheating, temperature light

Deal with it now

WTF it means

The engine is overheating, usually because of low coolant, a leak, a failed fan or a stuck thermostat.

What to do about it

Pull over and turn the engine off. Do NOT open the coolant cap while hot, as it can spray boiling liquid. Once cool, check the coolant level and top up; if it keeps happening, get it looked at.

For the record

A blue version of this symbol is the opposite: the engine is still cold. Take it easy until it goes out.
