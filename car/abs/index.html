ABS warning

Also called: anti-lock braking system, anti-lock brakes

Sort it soon

WTF it means

There's a fault in the anti-lock braking system. Your normal brakes still work, but the wheels could lock up in an emergency stop.

What to do about it

Drive carefully, leave extra stopping distance and get it checked soon. If the red brake light is on as well, stop: that's more serious.
