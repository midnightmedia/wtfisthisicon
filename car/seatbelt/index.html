Car dashboard · Red light

Seatbelt reminder

Also called: fasten seatbelt, belt minder

Deal with it now

WTF it means

Someone (driver or passenger) isn't wearing their seatbelt. It usually beeps too.

What to do about it

Buckle up. If everyone's belted in, check for a heavy bag on a passenger seat. The sensor can mistake it for a person.
