Car dashboard · Green light

Foot on a pedal (press the brake to start)

Also called: depress brake pedal, brake pedal reminder, push start reminder, foot on brake light

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

On cars with a start button, this is the car asking you to press the brake pedal. Most automatics won't start, or move out of Park, until you do. On a manual it's usually the clutch it wants pressed instead.

What to do about it

Press the brake firmly (or the clutch all the way down), then press the start button. If the light stays on while your foot is definitely on the brake, the brake light switch may have failed, which can also mean your brake lights aren't working. Get that checked soon.
