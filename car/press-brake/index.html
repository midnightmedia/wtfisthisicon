Car dashboard · Green light

Shoe in a circle (press the brake pedal)

Also called: press brake pedal, depress brake pedal, shift lock indicator, push start reminder, foot on brake light

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The car is asking you to press the brake pedal. On automatics with a start button, it won't start, or come out of Park, until you do. The same shoe in yellow usually means press the clutch instead (on manuals).

What to do about it

Press the brake firmly (or the clutch all the way down), then press the start button or move the gear lever. If the light stays on while your foot is definitely on the brake, the brake light switch may have failed, which can also mean your brake lights aren't working. Get that checked soon.

For the record

VW and Audi show it inside the same brackets as the brake warning light, so it's easy to mistake for a brake fault at a glance. Some makers call it the shift lock indicator.
