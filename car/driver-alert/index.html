Car dashboard · Amber light

Coffee cup (driver tiredness alert)

Also called: driver alert, attention assist, fatigue detection, driver drowsiness warning, rest recommended, take a break light

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The car thinks you're getting tired and suggests a break. It watches how you steer (small, jerky corrections are a classic sign) and how long you've been driving, and some cars use a camera that watches your eyes.

What to do about it

Stop for a break at the next safe place. The Highway Code recommends at least 15 minutes every two hours. Caffeine and a short nap help for a while; opening the window and turning up the radio don't.

For the record

It's been required on every new car sold in the EU since July 2024, which is why it's suddenly everywhere. Yes, the car is offering you a coffee.
