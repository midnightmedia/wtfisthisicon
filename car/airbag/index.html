Car dashboard · Red light

Airbag warning

Also called: SRS, supplemental restraint system, airbag fault

Sort it soon

WTF it means

There's a fault in the airbag system. In a crash, the airbags (and sometimes the seatbelt pre-tensioners) might not deploy.

What to do about it

You can still drive the car, but get it checked as soon as possible. Until it's fixed, you're less protected.
