Car dashboard · Amber light

Traction / stability control

Also called: ESC, ESP, TCS, DSC, VSC, stability control, skid light

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

If it FLASHES while you're driving, the system is actively stopping your wheels from slipping. That's normal on wet, icy or loose roads. If it stays ON, there's a fault and the system is off.

What to do about it

Flashing: ease off the accelerator and drive gently. Constantly on: you can drive, but carefully, and get it checked soon.
