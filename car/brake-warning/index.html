Car dashboard · Red light

Brake system warning

Also called: brake fluid warning, BRAKE light

Deal with it now

WTF it means

Either the handbrake is still on, or there's a problem with the braking system (most often low brake fluid).

What to do about it

Check the handbrake is fully released. If the light stays on, stop somewhere safe and check the brake fluid level. Don't drive on if the brakes feel soft or spongy.
