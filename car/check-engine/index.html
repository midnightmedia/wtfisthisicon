Car dashboard · Amber light

Check engine light

Also called: engine management light, EML, MIL, malfunction indicator lamp, CEL, service engine soon

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The engine's computer has spotted a problem with the engine or emissions system. It could be anything from a loose fuel cap to a misfiring engine. Quite a range for one little submarine.

What to do about it

Steady: get it scanned at a garage (or with a cheap OBD-II reader) soon. FLASHING: the engine is misfiring badly. Slow down and get it checked immediately to avoid damaging the catalytic converter.

For the record

A loose fuel cap is a classic cause. Tighten it and the light may go off by itself after a few drives.
