Rear fog light

Also called: rear fog lamp

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The rear fog light is on. It's a very bright red light on the back of the car.

What to do about it

Only use it when visibility is really poor (roughly under 100 m / 330 ft). Left on in clear weather, it dazzles the driver behind and can hide your brake lights. They won't thank you for it.
