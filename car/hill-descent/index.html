Car dashboard · Green light

Hill descent control

Also called: HDC, downhill assist, descent control

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Hill descent control is on. The car brakes automatically so you crawl slowly down steep, slippery slopes.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's meant for off-road or very steep descents. Flashing usually means it's actively braking.
