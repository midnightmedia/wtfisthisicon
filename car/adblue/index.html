Car dashboard · Amber light

AdBlue / DEF low

Also called: diesel exhaust fluid, DEF, AdBlue, SCR

Sort it soon

WTF it means

Diesel cars with AdBlue: the tank of exhaust-cleaning fluid is running low.

What to do about it

Top up with AdBlue / DEF (sold at fuel stations). Don't ignore it: when the tank runs dry, most cars won't restart until you refill. They're surprisingly strict about it.
