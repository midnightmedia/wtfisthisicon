Car dashboard · Amber light

Water in fuel (diesel)

Also called: water in fuel filter, fuel filter water warning, WIF, drain fuel filter

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The fuel filter's water trap has collected water. Diesel picks up water from condensation (and the odd bad batch of fuel), and the filter catches it before it reaches the injection system, which really doesn't like water.

What to do about it

Get the filter drained or replaced soon, at a garage unless you know your way round a diesel fuel system. Drive gently until then. Ignoring it can damage the high-pressure fuel pump and injectors, which is an eye-watering bill.

For the record

Some makes show a fuel filter with a drop or wavy lines instead of the pump. Same message either way.
