Car dashboard · Green light

Auto start-stop

Also called: start/stop, stop-start, idle stop, ISG

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Green: the engine has switched itself off to save fuel while you're stopped, and it restarts when you lift off the brake or press the clutch. Crossed out or amber: the feature is off or unavailable (cold engine, low battery, air-con working hard…).

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's normal, even if the sudden silence at the lights takes some getting used to. Most cars have a button with this symbol to disable it for the current trip.
