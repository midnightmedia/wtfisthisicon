Car dashboard · Amber light

Collision warning or emergency braking off

Also called: forward collision warning, FCW, AEB, autonomous emergency braking, pre-collision system, PCS, front assist, city safety

Sort it soon

WTF it means

This is the car's forward collision warning and automatic emergency braking. Flashing red with beeps means the car thinks you're about to hit something ahead. Steady amber (often with OFF) means the system is switched off or can't see, usually because the camera behind the windscreen or the radar in the front grille is dirty, iced up or blocked.

What to do about it

Flashing red: brake now. Steady amber: clean the windscreen in front of the mirror and the badge or panel on the front grille, and check it hasn't been switched off in the settings. If it stays on, get it checked, especially after a new windscreen, as the camera needs recalibrating.
