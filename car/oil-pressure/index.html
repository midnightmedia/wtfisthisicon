Car dashboard · Red light

Oil pressure warning

Also called: low oil pressure, oil can light, engine oil warning

Deal with it now

WTF it means

The engine has lost oil pressure, so its moving parts aren't being lubricated properly. Running an engine without oil pressure can wreck it in minutes.

What to do about it

Pull over safely and switch the engine off as soon as you can. Once it's cooled, check the oil level with the dipstick and top up. If the level is fine and the light stays on, don't drive. Get it towed.

For the record

It's an oil can, though most people see a genie lamp. If the same symbol is amber/yellow (sometimes with a wavy line), many cars mean 'oil level low' rather than 'no pressure'. That's less urgent, but top up soon.
