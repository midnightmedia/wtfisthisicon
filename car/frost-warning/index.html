Car dashboard · Amber light

Frost / ice warning

Also called: ice warning, low outside temperature, black ice warning

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The outside temperature is close to freezing (usually 3–4 °C / 37–39 °F or below), so there could be ice on the road.

What to do about it

Drive gently and watch for black ice, especially on bridges and shaded roads.

For the record

On the climate-control panel (not the dashboard), the same snowflake usually means the air conditioning button. One snowflake, two jobs.
