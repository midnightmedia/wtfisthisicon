Car dashboard · Red light

Parking brake on

Also called: handbrake, electronic parking brake, EPB

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The parking brake (handbrake) is on. As dashboard mysteries go, this is one of the gentler ones.

What to do about it

Release the handbrake before driving. If it's off and the light is still on (or flashing), the electronic parking brake may have a fault. Get it checked.
