Car dashboard · Red light

Door open

Also called: door ajar, door warning

Deal with it now

WTF it means

One or more doors (sometimes the boot or bonnet) isn't fully shut.

What to do about it

Stop and close the door properly before setting off. Some cars show exactly which door on the display.
