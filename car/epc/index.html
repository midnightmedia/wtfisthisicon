EPC light

Also called: electronic power control, EPC warning, throttle fault light, limp mode

Sort it soon

WTF it means

EPC stands for Electronic Power Control, and it's a VW, Audi, Skoda, Seat and Cupra thing. It means a fault in the system that controls the engine's power, often the throttle, the accelerator pedal sensor or the brake light switch. The car may cut power to protect itself (limp mode).

What to do about it

Drive gently to a garage and have the fault codes read. If the car feels badly down on power, avoid motorways. Check your brake lights work, as a failed brake light switch is a common (and cheap) cause.

For the record

It often turns up with the check engine light. EPC lighting up for a moment when you start the car is normal.
