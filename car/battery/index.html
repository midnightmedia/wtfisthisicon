Car dashboard · Red light

Battery / charging warning

Also called: charging system warning, alternator light, battery light

Deal with it now

WTF it means

The battery isn't being charged. Usually the alternator, its drive belt or the wiring has failed. The car is running on battery power alone and will die when it's flat.

What to do about it

Turn off everything you don't need (heater, radio, heated seats) and drive straight to a garage or somewhere safe. Avoid switching the engine off: it might not start again.

For the record

Seeing it briefly when you turn the key, before the engine starts, is perfectly normal.
