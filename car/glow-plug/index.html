Car dashboard · Amber light

Glow plug (diesel)

Also called: diesel preheat, heater plug

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Diesel cars only: the glow plugs are warming up the engine so it will start. It's normal for it to light up when you turn the key. And no, it isn't a Slinky.

What to do about it

Wait for it to go out before you start the engine. If it FLASHES while you're driving, or stays on, there's an engine fault, so get it checked.
