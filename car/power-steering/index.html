Car dashboard · Red light

Power steering warning

Also called: EPS, electric power steering, EPAS, steering fault

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The power steering has a fault. The steering still works, but it may suddenly become very heavy.

What to do about it

Drive carefully, especially at low speeds and when parking, and get it checked soon. Some cars show it in amber for a less serious fault.
