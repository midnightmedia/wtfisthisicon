Car dashboard · Green light

Cruise control

Also called: speed control, adaptive cruise control, ACC

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Cruise control is on. White or grey usually means it's ready; green means it's active and holding a speed.

What to do about it

Tap the brake or the cancel button to switch it off.
