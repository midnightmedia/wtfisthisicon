Car dashboard · Amber light

Washer fluid low

Also called: windscreen washer, windshield washer fluid

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The windscreen washer bottle is running low. That's a windscreen with a fountain, if you squint.

What to do about it

Top up with screenwash (not just water in winter, because it freezes). The filler cap usually has the same symbol on it.
