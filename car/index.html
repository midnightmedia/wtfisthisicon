Car dashboard warning lights

Warning lights that glow at you. 36 of them, decoded.

Red = stop safely & check now Amber = get it checked soon Green / blue = a system is on, all fine

Oil pressure warning

The engine has lost oil pressure, so its moving parts aren't being lubricated properly.

Deal with it now

Engine temperature warning

The engine is overheating, usually because of low coolant, a leak, a failed fan or a stuck thermostat.

Deal with it now

Battery / charging warning

The battery isn't being charged.

Deal with it now

Brake system warning

Either the handbrake is still on, or there's a problem with the braking system (most often low brake fluid).

Deal with it now

Seatbelt reminder

Someone (driver or passenger) isn't wearing their seatbelt.

Deal with it now

Door open

One or more doors (sometimes the boot or bonnet) isn't fully shut.

Deal with it now

Airbag warning

There's a fault in the airbag system.

Sort it soon

Power steering warning

The power steering has a fault.

Sort it soon

Check engine light

The engine's computer has spotted a problem with the engine or emissions system.

Sort it soon

Tyre pressure warning

At least one tyre is significantly under-inflated.

Sort it soon

ABS warning

There's a fault in the anti-lock braking system.

Sort it soon

Brake pad wear

Your brake pads have worn down to the sensor and need replacing.

Sort it soon

Low fuel

You're running low on fuel.

Sort it soon

Diesel particulate filter (DPF)

The diesel particulate filter in the exhaust is clogging up with soot.

Sort it soon

AdBlue / DEF low

Diesel cars with AdBlue: the tank of exhaust-cleaning fluid is running low.

Sort it soon

Gearbox / transmission warning

There's a problem with the gearbox (usually an automatic).

Sort it soon

Bulb failure

One of the exterior lights (headlight, brake light, indicator, number plate light) has blown.

Sort it soon

Parking brake on

The parking brake (handbrake) is on.

Relax, it's fine

Traction / stability control

If it FLASHES while you're driving, the system is actively stopping your wheels from slipping.

Relax, it's fine

Traction control OFF

The traction / stability control has been switched off, usually because someone pressed the button.

Relax, it's fine

Glow plug (diesel)

Diesel cars only: the glow plugs are warming up the engine so it will start.

Relax, it's fine

Washer fluid low

The windscreen washer bottle is running low.

Relax, it's fine

Frost / ice warning

The outside temperature is close to freezing (usually 3–4 °C / 37–39 °F or below), so there could be ice on the road.

Relax, it's fine

Service due

Your car is due its scheduled service.

Relax, it's fine

Key not detected / immobiliser

The car can't detect your key, often because the fob battery is dying or the key is out of range.

Relax, it's fine

Lane assist

Green or white: lane assist is on and can see the lane markings.

Relax, it's fine

Dipped headlights (low beam)

Your normal headlights are on.

Relax, it's fine

Full beam (high beam)

Your full-beam / high-beam headlights are on.

Relax, it's fine

Front fog lights

Your front fog lights are on: the low, wide beams meant for when you genuinely can't see far ahead.

Relax, it's fine

Rear fog light

The rear fog light is on.

Relax, it's fine

Sidelights / parking lights

Your sidelights (the small parking/position lights) are on, but your headlights aren't.

Relax, it's fine

Indicators / turn signals

Your indicator is on (one arrow flashing), or your hazard lights are on (both flashing).

Relax, it's fine

Cruise control

Cruise control is on.

Relax, it's fine

Eco mode

Eco driving mode is on.

Relax, it's fine

Auto start-stop

Green: the engine has switched itself off to save fuel while you're stopped, and it restarts when you lift off the brake or press the clutch.

Relax, it's fine

Hill descent control

Hill descent control is on.

Relax, it's fine
