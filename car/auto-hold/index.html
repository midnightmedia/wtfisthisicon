Car dashboard · Green light

Auto Hold (A in a circle with brackets)

Also called: AUTO HOLD, brake hold, automatic vehicle hold, AVH, hold assist

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Auto Hold is holding the brakes for you. When you stop at lights or in a queue, you can take your foot off the brake and the car stays put, then it lets go when you press the accelerator. Green usually means it's holding the car right now.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. There's usually a button by the gear lever or the parking brake switch to turn it on or off. Most cars only allow it with your seatbelt on and the door shut.

For the record

Not to be confused with auto start-stop, which is an A inside a circular arrow and switches the engine off, not the brakes on.
