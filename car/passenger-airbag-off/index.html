Car dashboard · Amber light

Passenger airbag off

Also called: PASSENGER AIRBAG OFF, airbag deactivated, passenger air bag off light, airbag off switch

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The front passenger airbag is switched off. Either someone has turned it off with the key switch (usually in the glovebox or on the end of the dashboard), or the seat has decided nobody heavy enough is sitting there, which is normal for an empty seat or a small child.

What to do about it

If you have a rear-facing baby seat in the front, it must be OFF: it's illegal to fit one in front of an active airbag. If an adult is sitting there and it says OFF, check the switch is set to ON and that they're sitting back properly in the seat. If it still says OFF, get the seat sensor checked.

For the record

There's usually a matching ON light. Seeing OFF next to an empty seat is normal on cars that detect who's sitting there.
