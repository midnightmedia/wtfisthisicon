Car dashboard · Amber light

Blind spot warning

Also called: blind spot monitoring, BSM, BLIS, blind spot information system, side assist, lane change assist

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

This is the car's blind spot monitoring. Radar in the rear bumper watches the lanes beside and behind you, and lights a symbol in (or next to) the door mirror when something's lurking where you can't see it. On the dashboard, an amber symbol (often with OFF) means the system is switched off or can't see.

What to do about it

If the light in the mirror is on or flashing, don't change lanes until it goes out. If the dashboard warning stays on, check the rear bumper corners for dirt, snow, stickers or a bike rack, which block the radar. Still on? It may be switched off in the settings, or need a garage.

For the record

It's a helper, not a replacement for a look over your shoulder.
