Car dashboard · Amber light

Diesel particulate filter (DPF)

Also called: DPF warning, particulate filter blocked, soot filter

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The diesel particulate filter in the exhaust is clogging up with soot. It normally burns this off on longer, faster drives, but lots of short trips stop that happening. In short, your car fancies a proper run out.

What to do about it

Drive at a steady 60+ km/h (40+ mph) for 15–20 minutes on a motorway or main road so it can regenerate. If the light doesn't clear, see a garage.
