Car dashboard · Amber light

Traction control OFF

Also called: ESC OFF, ESP OFF, TCS OFF, DSC OFF

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The traction / stability control has been switched off, usually because someone pressed the button. A rare symbol that actually spells it out.

What to do about it

Press the button with the same symbol (often near the gear lever or on the dash) to switch it back on. Only turn it off deliberately, for example to get out of snow or mud.
