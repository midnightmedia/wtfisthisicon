Car dashboard · Amber light

Brake pad wear

Also called: brake pads worn, brake lining

Sort it soon

WTF it means

Your brake pads have worn down to the sensor and need replacing.

What to do about it

Book a brake service soon. Leave it too long and you'll start damaging the brake discs, which turns a routine job into a pricier one.
