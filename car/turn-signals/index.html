Car dashboard · Green light

Indicators / turn signals

Also called: blinkers, turn signal, hazard lights

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Your indicator is on (one arrow flashing), or your hazard lights are on (both flashing). The clicking is there in case the flashing didn't get your attention.

What to do about it

If it's flashing much faster than usual, one of the indicator bulbs has probably blown. The frantic blinking is the car's way of telling you.
