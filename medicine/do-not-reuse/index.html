Medicine & medical

Crossed-out 2 (single use only)

Also called: do not re-use, do not reuse, single use, single-use only, use only once, disposable, ISO 7000-1051

WTF it means

Single use: use it once, then throw it away. Common on needles, lancets, syringes, dressings, catheters, test kits and some masks.

What to do about it

Don't wash, sterilise or use it again, even on the same person: it may not stay clean, sharp or safe. Put used needles and lancets in a sharps bin.

For the record

Whatever it looks like, it doesn't mean 'two' of anything or 'use within 2 days': the 2 stands for 'a second time'. 'Single patient use' is a different label, meaning one person can use it more than once.
