Medicine & medical

Solid factory (manufacturer)

Also called: manufacturer, made by, legal manufacturer, factory symbol, ISO 7000-3082

WTF it means

The name and address next to the filled-in factory belong to the manufacturer: the company legally responsible for the product. You'll see it on medical devices such as plasters, thermometers, home tests, condoms and first-aid kits.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's who to contact, or to name when you report a problem, if the product is faulty.

For the record

Spot the difference: an empty, outline factory is a different symbol, meaning date of manufacture, with a date next to it instead of an address. Products made outside Europe often also list an EU representative, marked 'EC REP'.
