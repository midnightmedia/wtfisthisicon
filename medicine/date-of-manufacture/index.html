Medicine & medical

Outline factory (date of manufacture)

Also called: date of manufacture, manufactured on, production date, MFG, ISO 7000-2497

WTF it means

The date next to the outline (not filled-in) factory is when the product was made. It's usually written year first, like 2026-03 or 2026-03-14.

What to do about it

Nothing to do: it isn't an expiry date. Look for the hourglass for the date to use it by.

For the record

Don't mix it up with the solid black factory, which marks the manufacturer's name and address. Same factory, different job, depending entirely on the colouring-in.
