Medicine & medical

Closed eye or Zzz (may cause drowsiness)

Also called: may cause drowsiness, drowsiness, may make you sleepy, do not drive, auxiliary label, pharmacy sticker

WTF it means

The medicine may make you sleepy or less alert. The picture stickers are mostly used by US and Canadian pharmacies, often with a note that alcohol makes it worse.

What to do about it

Until you know how it affects you, don't drive, cycle, use machinery or do anything that needs you fully alert, and avoid alcohol. If you feel drowsy, don't drive.

For the record

In the UK it's printed as words on the pharmacy label, such as 'Warning: This medicine may make you sleepy'. French boxes use a car in a coloured triangle.
