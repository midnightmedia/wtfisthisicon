Japanese toilets

Pulsating / massage wash (マッサージ)

Also called: マッサージ (massāji), マッサージ洗浄 (massāji senjō), PULSATE, pulsating, massage

WTF it means

Makes the spray pulse between strong and soft, which some people find more pleasant. Japan has given this a lot of thought.

What to do about it

Press it during a rear wash to start the pulsing and again to stop it. 止 (stop) ends the wash.

For the record

How strong the pulse feels depends on the pressure setting: big at +, barely noticeable at −.
