Japanese toilets

Small flush (小)

Also called: 小 (shō), 流す 小 (nagasu shō), 便器洗浄 小 (benki senjō shō), half flush, light flush, eco flush, LIGHT FLUSH

WTF it means

A shorter flush with less water: for a wee and a little paper. The standard symbol is a smaller swirl with fewer drops, and buttons and levers usually say 小, 'small'.

What to do about it

Use it after a wee. If it doesn't clear everything, press 大 (big) as well.

For the record

TOTO's guidance for one of its toilets: small flush for a wee and up to about 3 metres of single-ply paper, big flush for anything more. Measuring is optional.
