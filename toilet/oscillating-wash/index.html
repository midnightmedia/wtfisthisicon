Japanese toilets

Oscillating wash (ムーブ)

Also called: ムーブ (mūbu), ムーブ洗浄 (mūbu senjō), OSCILLATE, oscillating, moving wash

WTF it means

Slides the nozzle back and forth while it sprays, so it washes a wider area rather than one spot. Think sprinkler, not jet.

What to do about it

Press it during a wash to switch it on, and again to change mode or turn it off. 止 (stop) ends the wash completely.

For the record

On TOTO remotes it shares a button with massage (OSCILLATE / PULSATE): each press steps through move, massage, both, then off, with a different beep for each.
