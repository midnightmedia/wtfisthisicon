Japanese toilets

Bidet / front wash (ビデ)

Also called: ビデ (bide), ビデ洗浄 (bide senjō), front wash, feminine wash, FRONT, bidet

WTF it means

The front wash, mainly for women: a softer, wider spray aimed further forward than the rear wash (some seats even use a separate nozzle for it). The standard pictogram is a seated person with a dotted fountain underneath.

What to do about it

Press it while sitting and press 止 (stop) to end it. If you only came in for a wee, you can ignore it completely.

For the record

In Japan 'bidet' means this front wash only; the whole seat is a washlet or 'shower toilet'. On some TOTO remotes the button reads ビデ / ワイド (FRONT / WIDE) and pressing it again gives a wider spray.
