Japanese toilets

Soft or wide spray (ソフト / ワイド)

Also called: おしりソフト (oshiri sofuto), ワイドビデ (waido bide), ソフト (sofuto), ワイド (waido), SOFT, WIDE, gentle wash

WTF it means

Gentler versions of the two washes: おしりソフト is a softer rear spray that 'wraps round', ワイドビデ a wider, gentler front spray.

What to do about it

On TOTO remotes they share a button with the normal washes (labelled おしり / ソフト and ビデ / ワイド): press it again during the wash to switch. Press 止 (stop) to end it.

For the record

There's no standard picture for this one, so many remotes just shrug and show the normal rear or bidet pictogram with ソフト (soft) or ワイド (wide) written next to it.
