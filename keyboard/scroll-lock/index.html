Scroll Lock (ScrLk)

Also called: Scroll Lock, ScrLk, Scr Lk, ScrLock, Rollen

WTF it means

A key from the early days of the PC that refuses to retire. When it's on, the arrow keys scroll the page instead of moving the cursor. These days practically only Microsoft Excel still cares. On laptops its light may be a padlock with an arrow.

What to do about it

Arrow keys in Excel scrolling the whole sheet instead of moving between cells, with "Scroll Lock" in the status bar at the bottom? Press ScrLk to turn it off. No such key on your laptop? Open the On-Screen Keyboard (search Start for "osk") and click ScrLk there.

For the record

On some cheap light-up keyboards, Scroll Lock switches the backlight on and off.
