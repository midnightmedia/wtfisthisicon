Keyboard symbols

Caps Lock

Also called: ⇪, Caps Lock, caps, capitals lock, caps lock light, A lock

WTF it means

Caps Lock makes every letter you type a CAPITAL until you press it again, which is how perfectly calm emails end up sounding like shouting. Numbers and symbols aren't affected. A little light on the key shows when it's on; on laptops the light is sometimes a padlock with an A.

What to do about it

Password suddenly "wrong"? Check Caps Lock first. On a Mac it needs a firm, slightly longer press, so you don't switch it on by accident. If you've set it to switch keyboard languages (System Settings › Keyboard), a quick tap does that instead.

For the record

Chromebooks don't have a Caps Lock key: that spot is a search key. Press Alt + Search to switch capitals on or off.
