Dictation key (microphone)

Also called: dictation, microphone key, mic key, F5, voice typing, Siri key

WTF it means

On newer Macs (F5), the microphone starts Dictation: speak, and your words get typed wherever the cursor is. On some models, pressing and holding it calls Siri instead.

What to do about it

Click in a text box, press the key and start talking. Press it again (or Esc) to stop. Say "comma" or "new line" for punctuation and layout. Yes, out loud. The Windows version is voice typing: Windows key + H.

For the record

On many Windows laptops, a microphone with a line through it is a different key: it mutes your mic for video calls, and its little light shows when you're muted.
