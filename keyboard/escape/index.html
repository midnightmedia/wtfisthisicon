Keyboard symbols

Escape key (Esc)

Also called: ⎋, Esc, Escape, esc key

WTF it means

Escape: cancel, close or back out of whatever just popped up, such as a dialog box, a menu, a full-screen video or a half-finished selection. The symbol is an arrow escaping from a circle, which is about as literal as icons get.

What to do about it

Stuck in full screen or a pop-up? Press Esc. For a frozen app, Option + Command + Esc opens Force Quit on a Mac, and Ctrl + Shift + Esc opens Task Manager on Windows.

For the record

Most keyboards just print "esc". The circle-and-arrow symbol mostly turns up in Mac menus and on some international keyboards. Some 2016–2019 MacBook Pros had no physical Esc key at all: it lived on the Touch Bar.
