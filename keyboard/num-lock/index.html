Keyboard symbols

Num Lock (padlock with a 1)

Also called: Num Lock, NumLk, Num Lk, number lock, numeric lock, number pad lock

WTF it means

Num Lock switches the number pad between typing numbers (light on) and working as arrow and navigation keys, using the little Home, End and arrow labels under the numbers (light off). On laptops its light is often a padlock with a 1.

What to do about it

Number pad moving the cursor instead of typing numbers? Press Num Lock. Laptop typing numbers when you press letters like U, I, O, J, K or L? Num Lock has switched on a number pad hidden inside the letter keys. Press Num Lock again (often Fn + a top-row key) to turn it off.

For the record

Mac keyboards don't have Num Lock: the key in that spot is called Clear, and the number pad always types numbers.
