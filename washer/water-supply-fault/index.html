Washing machine

Water supply problem (tap)

Also called: no water, tap light, water inlet error, check water supply, 4C error, IE error

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The machine isn't getting enough water. The tap may be off, the inlet hose kinked, the little filter in the hose blocked, or the water pressure too low.

What to do about it

Check the tap behind the machine is fully open and the hose isn't squashed, then restart. If it keeps happening, turn the tap off, unscrew the hose at the machine end and clean the mesh filter.

For the record

A classic after plumbing work or a freezing night, or when someone turned the tap off before going on holiday.
