Washing machine

Add garment / pause and add

Also called: add item, add wash, addwash, pause and add, add laundry, forgotten sock

WTF it means

Lets you pause the programme and add a forgotten item after it has started. The lone sock's second chance.

What to do about it

Press it and wait for the door to unlock (only possible while the water level and temperature are low). Add the item, close the door and press Start again.

For the record

After a certain point in the cycle, or above a certain temperature, the machine won't let you add anything.
