Washing machine

Fabric softener compartment

Also called: softener, conditioner, fabric conditioner, flower compartment

WTF it means

The flower (or star) compartment in the detergent drawer is for fabric softener or conditioner only. The machine flushes it in during the final rinse.

What to do about it

Pour softener up to the MAX line and no further, and never put detergent in here. Overfilling makes it siphon away too early.

For the record

The other compartments: I = prewash detergent, II = main wash detergent. Skip softener for towels and sportswear, as it reduces absorbency and breathability.
