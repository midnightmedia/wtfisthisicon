Washing machine

Hand wash / delicates programme

Also called: hand wash, delicates, silk, gentle

WTF it means

An extra-gentle programme that pretends to be you washing by hand: cool water, slow rocking movements and very little spinning.

What to do about it

Use it for lingerie, silk and delicate items, but check your manual before trusting it with hand-wash-only labels. A mesh bag adds extra protection.

For the record

Some machines use a feather, flower or butterfly for their delicates programme instead, just to keep things interesting.
