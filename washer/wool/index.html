Washing machine

Wool

Also called: woollens, wool programme, woolmark

WTF it means

A very gentle, cool programme for machine-washable wool and cashmere, with minimal drum movement and a low spin, so your jumper comes out the size it went in.

What to do about it

Use it for items with a washtub and two lines on the label, or marked 'machine washable wool', and use a wool detergent.

For the record

Machines with Woolmark approval have had this programme tested as safe even for some hand-wash wool.
