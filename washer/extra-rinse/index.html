Washing machine

Extra rinse

Also called: rinse plus, rinse+, additional rinse, allergy rinse

WTF it means

Adds one more rinse at the end of the programme to wash out more detergent. Handy if your skin has opinions about the stuff.

What to do about it

Turn it on for babies' clothes, sensitive skin or allergies, or if white detergent marks show up on dark clothes.

For the record

It costs extra water and time, so leave it off for everyday loads.
