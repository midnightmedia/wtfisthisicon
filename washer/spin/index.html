Washing machine

Spin

Also called: spin cycle, spin only, rpm, spin speed

WTF it means

The spin function or spin-speed setting. The drum whizzes round fast to fling the water out of the laundry.

What to do about it

Pick a high speed (1200–1400 rpm) for towels and cottons so they dry faster, and a lower one for delicates and synthetics to avoid creases.

For the record

On most machines, pressing the spin button cycles through the speeds. As a programme on its own, it spins the water out without washing.
