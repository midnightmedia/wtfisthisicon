Washing machine

Prewash

Also called: pre-wash, pre wash, compartment I

WTF it means

Adds a short extra wash before the main wash to loosen heavy dirt, like muddy sports kit or very dirty work clothes.

What to do about it

Only use it for genuinely filthy loads, and put a little detergent in the compartment marked I in the detergent drawer.

For the record

Detergent drawer cheat sheet, in Roman numerals for some reason: I = prewash, II = main wash, flower = fabric softener. Most loads only need II.
