Washing machine

Steam / anti-allergy

Also called: steam, allergy care, hygiene, anti-allergen, refresh

WTF it means

Adds steam to the wash to help kill bacteria and dust mites and loosen dirt. As a separate programme, it can also refresh and de-wrinkle dry clothes, which is as close as a washing machine gets to ironing.

What to do about it

Use it for bedding, baby clothes or allergy sufferers. Check the care labels first: steam is hot, so it's not for wool or delicates.

For the record

Steam programmes take longer and use more energy than a normal wash, so save them for when hygiene really matters.
