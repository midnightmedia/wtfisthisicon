Washing machine

No spin

Also called: spin off, spin stop, 0 rpm

WTF it means

The final spin is switched off, so the cycle ends after draining and the laundry comes out very wet.

What to do about it

Use it for drip-dry items or delicates that crease badly. Have a basin ready: it'll be soggy.

For the record

This matches the 'drip dry' symbols on care labels. Hang things up straight away.
