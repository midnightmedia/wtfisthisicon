Washing machine

Cotton

Also called: cottons, cotton programme, normal

WTF it means

The cotton programme: the sturdy, hard-working cycle for towels, bedding, T-shirts and other cotton or linen items.

What to do about it

Choose it for robust everyday cottons and set the temperature to match the care labels (usually 40 or 60 °C).

For the record

Cotton programmes use more drum movement and a faster spin than the synthetics or delicates programmes.
