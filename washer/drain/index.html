Washing machine

Drain

Also called: pump out, drain only, empty water, spin/drain

WTF it means

Pumps the water out of the drum, either on its own or as part of a spin/drain programme. The arrow pointing down is the water leaving.

What to do about it

Use it to empty the machine after rinse hold or after cancelling a programme halfway. The door usually unlocks once the water's gone.

For the record

If the machine won't drain, the pump filter (behind a small flap at the bottom front) is probably clogged with the usual suspects: fluff, coins or hair clips.
