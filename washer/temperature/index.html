Washing machine

Temperature

Also called: temp, °C, heat, water temperature

WTF it means

Sets how hot the wash water gets, usually from cold up to 90 °C.

What to do about it

Press it to cycle through the temperatures, and match the number in the washtub on your care labels. That number is a maximum.

For the record

Lower is often fine: modern detergents clean well at 30 °C, and every step down saves energy.
