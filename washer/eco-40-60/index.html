Washing machine

Eco 40-60

Also called: eco, eco programme, energy saving, eco 40-60 programme

WTF it means

The EU's standard energy-saving programme. It washes normally soiled cottons labelled for 40 °C or 60 °C, even mixed together, using as little energy and water as it possibly can.

What to do about it

Use it as your everyday programme for normal cotton loads. It's long (3 hours is normal) but cheap to run.

For the record

It saves energy by washing cooler for longer, so the water rarely reaches 40 or 60 °C, whatever the name says. The energy label on your machine is based on this programme.
