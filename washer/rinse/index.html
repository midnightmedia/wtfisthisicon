Washing machine

Rinse

Also called: rinse only, rinse cycle, rinse and spin

WTF it means

Runs a rinse with clean water, usually followed by a spin, without a full wash.

What to do about it

Use it to rinse out leftover detergent, freshen up swimwear, or add softener to a load that's already washed.

For the record

If your skin reacts to detergent, an extra rinse removes much of what's left in the fabric.
