Washing machine

Delay start / end time

Also called: delay end, time delay, finish in, ready in, delay timer, timer

WTF it means

Sets the machine to start later, or to finish at a time you choose, such as when you're home or on cheap night-time electricity.

What to do about it

Press it repeatedly to add hours, then press Start. The machine counts down and starts by itself.

For the record

For long delays, put detergent in the drawer rather than the drum, and avoid it for very smelly or damp loads.
