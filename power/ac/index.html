Chargers & plugs

AC: alternating current (wavy line)

Also called: alternating current, AC, ~, mains, IEC 60417-5032

WTF it means

The wavy line means alternating current: the kind of electricity that comes out of a wall socket. On a charger label it's usually next to the INPUT, e.g. '100–240V ~ 50/60Hz'.

What to do about it

Check the voltage range. '100–240V' means it works anywhere in the world with just a plug adaptor. If it only says 230V or 120V, it needs a voltage converter in countries with the other kind of mains.

For the record

50/60Hz is how many times a second the current swings back and forth: 50 in Europe, the UK, Australia and most of Asia; 60 in North America. Japan, just to keep things interesting, uses both, depending on which half of the country you're in.
