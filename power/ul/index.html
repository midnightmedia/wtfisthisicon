Chargers & plugs

UL Listed (UL in a circle)

Also called: UL, Underwriters Laboratories, UL Listed, cULus, UL mark

WTF it means

UL (originally Underwriters Laboratories), an independent testing lab, has tested samples of this product against US safety standards for fire and electric shock. A small 'c' on the left and 'us' on the right means it's certified for Canada and the US.

What to do about it

A good sign, especially on chargers and power strips. A smudged or oddly drawn UL mark on a cheap charger may be a fake. You can check the file number on UL's online database (Product iQ).

For the record

'UL Recognized' (a backwards-looking R and U, as if this weren't confusing enough) is different: it's for parts that go inside a product, not the finished item. ETL and CSA marks are equivalent safety listings.
