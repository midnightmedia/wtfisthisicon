Chargers & plugs

PSE mark (Japan)

Also called: PSE, Product Safety Electrical Appliance & Material, DENAN, Japanese electrical safety mark

WTF it means

Japan's compulsory electrical safety mark. The diamond version is for 'specified' higher-risk products (including AC adapters and chargers), which must be checked by an independent lab. The round version is for other electrical goods, which the maker tests itself.

What to do about it

Nothing to do outside Japan. In Japan it's illegal to sell mains-powered products such as chargers without it.

For the record

Since 2019, lithium-ion power banks sold in Japan must carry the round PSE mark too.
