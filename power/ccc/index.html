CCC mark (China)

Also called: China Compulsory Certificate, China Compulsory Certification, 3C, CCC

WTF it means

China Compulsory Certification: the product meets China's mandatory safety rules and can legally be sold in mainland China. You'll often spot it on chargers made for the global market.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It says nothing about approval where you live, so look for your own country's mark (UKCA or CE, UL, RCM, PSE…).

For the record

It's also nicknamed '3C', which is at least quicker to say. Not to be confused with the CE mark: different letters, different market.
