Chargers & plugs

Double insulated (square in a square)

Also called: Class II, Class 2, double insulation, reinforced insulation, IEC 60417-5172

WTF it means

Class II, or double insulated: the live parts are wrapped in two layers of insulation (or one extra-strong layer), so the device doesn't need an earth connection to be safe. That's why most phone chargers, laptop bricks and hairdryers get away with just two pins.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's perfectly safe without an earth. Do stop using it if the casing is cracked or damaged, though: that insulation is the whole point.

For the record

UK plugs on double-insulated items still have a third pin, but it's often plastic, because it's only there to open the shutters in the socket. More doorman than earth.
