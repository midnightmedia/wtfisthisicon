FCC logo

Also called: Federal Communications Commission, FCC mark, FCC ID, FCC Part 15

WTF it means

The US Federal Communications Commission's mark: the device has been checked so its radio interference stays within US limits, so it won't mess up radios, Wi-Fi or TV. It's not a safety mark.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. For electrical safety in the US, look for a safety listing such as UL, ETL or CSA instead.

For the record

Since 2017 the logo has been optional for many products, so a missing FCC logo doesn't mean much. Gadgets with radios (phones, wireless chargers) carry an 'FCC ID' code you can look up online to see their test reports and internal photos. Surprisingly good reading, if that's your sort of thing.
