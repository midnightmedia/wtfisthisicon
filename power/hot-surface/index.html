Chargers & plugs

Hot surface

Also called: caution hot surface, hot surface warning, do not touch, ISO 7010 W017, IEC 60417-5041

WTF it means

Warning: this surface gets hot enough to burn you during normal use.

What to do about it

Don't touch it while it's on or just after, don't cover it, and keep it away from anything that could melt or catch fire. Let it cool down before you handle it or put it away.

For the record

Some chargers, lamps and heaters carry this because they're meant to get hot. An ordinary phone charger that's too hot to hold isn't normal, though. Unplug it and replace it.
