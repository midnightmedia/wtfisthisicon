Chargers & plugs

For indoor use only (house)

Also called: indoor use only, not for outdoor use, dry locations only, IEC 60417-5957

WTF it means

The device is designed to be used indoors only. It isn't protected against rain, splashes, damp or condensation. A refreshingly literal symbol: it's a house.

What to do about it

Keep it inside and dry: not in the garden, on a boat or in a damp garage. For outdoor use, buy something with an IP rating such as IP44 or higher.

For the record

IP ratings are easy to decode. The first digit is protection against dust and objects, the second against water. IP44 = splash-proof, IPX7 = can survive being dunked. You'll also see the little house on fairy lights and extension leads.
