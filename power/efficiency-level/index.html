Chargers & plugs

Efficiency level (Roman numeral in a circle)

Also called: efficiency mark, International Efficiency Marking Protocol, Level VI, Level V, DoE Level VI, energy efficiency level

WTF it means

Shows how energy-efficient an external power supply is, under the International Efficiency Marking Protocol. Higher is better: Level VI (6) wastes less power both while charging and while plugged in with nothing attached. And yes, it's in Roman numerals. On a charger.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. Level VI has been the minimum in the US since 2016, and the EU's rules are similar. Level V or lower just means an older, slightly less efficient design, which is still fine to use.

For the record

The numeral is an efficiency grade, not a voltage or a version number. A small Level VI charger may draw no more than 0.1 W when nothing is connected.
