Chargers & plugs

Safety isolating transformer (two overlapping circles)

Also called: safety isolating transformer, isolating transformer, safety transformer, SELV

WTF it means

The charger contains a safety isolating transformer: the dangerous mains side is completely separated from the low-voltage output, so the end you touch (the USB socket, cable or connector) can't become live even if something fails. It is not, despite appearances, a Venn diagram.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's a good thing to see on a charger. Still, stop using any charger with a cracked case or scorched pins, or that gets too hot to hold.

For the record

Small extra marks added to the two circles (such as a tiny fuse) describe how the transformer copes with a short circuit or overload.
