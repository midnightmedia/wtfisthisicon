Chargers & plugs

Dangerous voltage (lightning bolt in a triangle)

Also called: electric shock hazard, high voltage, electricity warning, danger of death, ISO 7010 W012

WTF it means

Warning: dangerous voltage inside, enough to give a serious electric shock. On chargers and power supplies it usually sits next to 'Do not open' or 'No user-serviceable parts inside'.

What to do about it

Don't open the casing, even when it's unplugged. Parts inside (capacitors) can stay charged for a while. If it's damaged, replace it rather than repair it.

For the record

The yellow-and-black triangle version is the safety sign you'll see on substations, fuse boxes and electrical cupboards.
