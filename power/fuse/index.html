Chargers & plugs

Fuse (rectangle with a line through it)

Also called: fuse, fuse rating, T3.15A, slow-blow fuse, IEC 60417-5016

WTF it means

Marks a fuse: a thin wire that heroically melts and cuts the power if too much current flows, to prevent a fire. It's usually printed next to its rating, e.g. 'T3.15AL 250V' = a time-delay (slow-blow) fuse of 3.15 amps, low breaking capacity, rated for up to 250 volts.

What to do about it

If the device is completely dead, a blown fuse is a likely suspect. Only ever replace it with exactly the same rating and type, never a bigger one or a bit of wire. If a new fuse blows straight away, there's a fault: stop and get it repaired.

For the record

F = fast-acting, T = time-lag (slow-blow, rides out the surge at switch-on). UK plugs have their own fuse too: usually 3A for small appliances and 13A for kettles and heaters. Sealed phone chargers have an internal fuse you can't replace.
