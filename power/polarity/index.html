Chargers & plugs

Polarity (centre-positive / centre-negative)

Also called: centre positive, center positive, centre negative, center negative, tip positive, barrel plug polarity, DC polarity

WTF it means

Shows the polarity of a round barrel-style DC plug. The dot is the centre (the inside of the plug), and the C-shaped ring is the outer sleeve. Whichever sign the dot is joined to is the centre: dot to + (as drawn here) = centre-positive, the most common; dot to − = centre-negative.

What to do about it

When replacing a power adapter, this symbol must match exactly, along with the voltage, the plug size and enough amps. Get the polarity wrong and it can damage a device the instant you plug it in.

For the record

Guitar effects pedals are the classic exception: most use 9V centre-negative, which is why a random router or keyboard adapter can fry them.
