Chargers & plugs

Earth / ground (protective earth)

Also called: Class I, protective earth, PE, ground, earth terminal, IEC 60417-5019, IEC 60417-5017

WTF it means

Marks the earth (ground) connection. Inside a circle, it's the protective earth of a Class I appliance: the metal casing is connected to earth, so if a live wire comes loose inside, the fuse or breaker trips instead of the case becoming live.

What to do about it

Devices with this symbol must be plugged into a properly earthed socket with the earth pin intact. Never cut the pin off or use an adaptor that loses it. If you're wiring a plug, the green-and-yellow wire (green or bare copper in North America) goes to this terminal.

For the record

Without the circle, the same 'upside-down Christmas tree' is a general earth point. A version with slanted lines like a rake marks a connection to the metal frame (chassis).
