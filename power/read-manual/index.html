Chargers & plugs

Read the instructions (open book)

Also called: refer to manual, read operating instructions, see user guide, consult the documentation, ISO 7000-1641

WTF it means

There's important information in the instructions or safety leaflet, usually about how to use or connect the product safely.

What to do about it

Dig out the leaflet that came with it, wherever it ended up (or look it up online), before using it. That goes double for anything that plugs into the mains.

For the record

A white book in a blue circle (ISO 7010 M002) is stronger: you MUST read the manual. A triangle with an exclamation mark beside it means 'caution: check the documentation'.
