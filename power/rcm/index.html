Chargers & plugs

RCM mark (tick in a circle, in a triangle)

Also called: Regulatory Compliance Mark, RCM, C-Tick, A-Tick, Australian compliance mark

WTF it means

The Regulatory Compliance Mark: the product meets Australian and New Zealand rules for electrical safety and radio interference. A tick of approval from down under, basically.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. In Australia or New Zealand, it's a good sign a charger was made to be sold legally there, so be wary of mains chargers without it.

For the record

It replaced the older C-Tick (a C with a tick inside) and A-Tick marks, which were phased out by 2016.
