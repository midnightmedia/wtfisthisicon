Boiler and heating symbols

Radiators, taps, flames & dials. 16 of them, decoded.

Lockout / reset (flame with a cross)

The boiler tried to light and couldn't, or spotted a fault, so it has shut itself down safely and 'locked out'.

Low water pressure

The water pressure inside your heating system has dropped too low, so the boiler has stopped to protect itself.

Service / spanner symbol

Depending on your boiler, the spanner means the annual service is due, the boiler is in engineer/service mode, or (usually shown with a code) there's a fault that needs an engineer.

Central heating (radiator symbol)

On a boiler or heating control, the radiator means central heating: the hot water pumped round your radiators (or underfloor pipes).

Hot water (tap symbol)

The tap means hot water for your taps, showers and baths, as opposed to the heating.

Flame (burner on)

The boiler's burner is lit: it's burning gas (or oil) right now to heat water.

Frost protection (snowflake)

Frost protection: the heating stays off, but switches itself on if the house (or the boiler) gets close to freezing, typically around 5°C, so pipes don't freeze and burst.

ECO / pre-heat (combi boilers)

On most combi boilers this controls 'pre-heat': whether the boiler keeps a little water hot inside so hot water reaches the tap sooner.

Boost (+1 hour)

Turns the heating (or hot water) on right now for a fixed time, usually 30 minutes to an hour (more presses for 2 or 3 hours), whatever the schedule says.

Flame on the thermostat (calling for heat)

The room thermostat is telling the boiler (or heat pump) to heat, because the room is colder than the temperature you've set.

Wavy lines going up (heat on)

Three rising wavy lines are the general 'heat' symbol.

Radiator valve numbers (TRV)

The numbered head on the side of a radiator is a thermostatic radiator valve (TRV).

Hot water cylinder / immersion heater

A tall tank is a hot water cylinder: stored hot water for taps and showers, used with system and regular boilers and heat pumps (not combis).

Holiday mode (suitcase)

Holiday (or away) mode pauses your normal heating schedule while you're away, holding the house at a low temperature (often 5–15°C, you choose) until the date you return.
