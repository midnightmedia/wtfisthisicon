Boilers & heating

Boost (+1 hour)

Also called: boost, +1h, +1 hr, one hour boost, extra hour, override

WTF it means

Turns the heating (or hot water) on right now for a fixed time, usually 30 minutes to an hour (more presses for 2 or 3 hours), whatever the schedule says. Then it goes back to the normal timetable by itself.

What to do about it

Press it when you're cold or need hot water outside the usual times. Press it again (or cancel) to stop early.

For the record

Not the same as 'Advance', which jumps to the next on/off period in the schedule rather than adding a fixed time. Smart thermostats usually let you pick the boost length and temperature in the app.
