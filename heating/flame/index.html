Boilers & heating

Flame (burner on)

Also called: flame, burner on, boiler firing, burner lit, ignition, fire

WTF it means

The boiler's burner is lit: it's burning gas (or oil) right now to heat water. It comes and goes as the boiler fires up and turns down, which is perfectly normal.

What to do about it

Nothing to do: it's just a status light. If it never appears and you've no heating or hot water, check the boiler has power, the pressure is OK and your other gas appliances work. Then look for a fault code.

For the record

On a room thermostat, a flame means 'asking the boiler for heat' instead. A carbon monoxide alarm going off, a lazy yellow or orange flame, or soot marks around the boiler mean: switch it off, open windows, get outside and call the gas emergency line (0800 111 999 in Great Britain), and see a doctor if anyone feels unwell.
