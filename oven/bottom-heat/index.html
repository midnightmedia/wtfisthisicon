Oven, hob & microwave

Bottom heat only

Also called: lower heat, base heat, bottom element, bottom heat

WTF it means

Only the element under the oven floor is on, so all the heat comes from below.

What to do about it

Use it to crisp up a pastry or pizza base at the end of cooking, or for slow cooking and water baths. Put the food on a low shelf.

For the record

Soggy-bottomed pie? Give it the last 10 minutes or so on this setting. Nobody need ever know.
