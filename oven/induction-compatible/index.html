Oven, hob & microwave

Induction-compatible (coil symbol)

Also called: induction symbol, induction suitable, induction ready, induction coil

WTF it means

Found on the bottom of pans and their packaging, it means the pan has a magnetic base and will work on an induction hob. Not a telephone cord, whatever it looks like.

What to do about it

No coil symbol on your pan? Hold a fridge magnet against the base. If it sticks firmly, the pan will work on induction.

For the record

The coil stands for the copper coil under the glass, which makes the magnetic field that heats the pan directly.
