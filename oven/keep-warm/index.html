Oven, hob & microwave

Keep warm / plate warming

Also called: keep warm, plate warmer, warming, hold warm, warm plates

WTF it means

A low-temperature setting, well below normal cooking temperatures, for warming plates or keeping cooked food warm without cooking it any further.

What to do about it

Put the plates or the finished food in and keep the door shut. Don't use it to cook raw food, and don't leave food sitting in it for hours.

For the record

Some ovens split this into two symbols, as if one wasn't puzzling enough: a stack of plates for plate warming and a dish with wavy lines for keeping food warm.
