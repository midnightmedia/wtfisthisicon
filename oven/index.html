Oven, hob and microwave symbols

Fans, squiggles & lines. 32 of them, decoded.

AUTO flashing on the clock

The oven clock is in automatic (timed) mode, or there's been a power cut and it's waiting for you to set the time.

Sort it soon

Residual heat 'H'

That cooking zone is switched off but still hot enough to burn you.

Relax, it's fine

No pan / unsuitable pan (induction)

The induction hob can't find a suitable pan on that zone.

Relax, it's fine

Conventional (top & bottom heat)

The classic oven setting: heating elements in the roof and the floor of the oven, and no fan.

Top heat only

Only the element in the roof of the oven is on, giving gentle heat from above.

Bottom heat only

Only the element under the oven floor is on, so all the heat comes from below.

Plain fan (defrost or fan oven)

The fan at the back of the oven spins.

Fan oven (true fan / fan forced)

The everyday fan oven setting.

Fan-assisted (top & bottom heat + fan)

The top and bottom elements are on and the fan spreads their heat around.

Pizza setting (fan + bottom heat)

The bottom element and the fan work together: strong heat from below plus moving hot air, so the base goes crisp while the toppings cook.

Full grill

The grill element in the roof is on across its full width.

Half grill (eco grill)

Only the middle part of the grill element heats up.

Fan grill

The grill element and the fan work together.

Defrost

Defrost mode.

Oven light

Switches on the light inside the oven, without any heating.

Self-clean (pyrolytic or steam clean)

The oven's own cleaning programme.

Keep warm / plate warming

A low-temperature setting, well below normal cooking temperatures, for warming plates or keeping cooked food warm without cooking it any further.

Steam

A steam-assisted setting.

Dough proving

A very gentle warm setting (around 30–40°C) that helps yeast dough rise faster and more reliably than a cool kitchen counter.

Rapid preheat / heating up

Rapid preheat uses extra elements (and usually the fan) to get the oven up to temperature faster.

Minute minder (timer bell)

A simple countdown timer that beeps when the time is up.

Cooking time / cooking on (saucepan)

On many oven clocks the saucepan (or 'cookpot') is the cooking-time button: set how long to cook and the oven switches itself off at the end.

End time (auto switch-off)

Sets the clock time you want cooking to finish.

Manual mode (hand symbol)

Takes the oven out of timed (automatic) mode so it works normally from the knobs.

Child lock / control lock

The controls (and on some models the door) are locked so nobody can switch the oven or hob on by accident.

Which ring this knob controls

A tiny map of the hob, printed next to each knob.

Dual zone / extended ring

This cooking zone has an extra outer ring.

Power boost 'P'

Boost is on.

Induction-compatible (coil symbol)

Found on the bottom of pans and their packaging, it means the pan has a magnetic base and will work on an induction hob.

Microwave power

Microwave mode, and the button for choosing how strong it is.

Microwave + grill combination

Microwaves and the grill work at the same time.

Microwave defrost

The defrost programme.
