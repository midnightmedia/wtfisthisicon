Oven, hob & microwave

Pizza setting (fan + bottom heat)

Also called: pizza, pizza setting, base heat with fan, bottom heat and fan, base crisp

WTF it means

The bottom element and the fan work together: strong heat from below plus moving hot air, so the base goes crisp while the toppings cook.

What to do about it

Use it for pizza, tarts, quiche and anything with a base that mustn't go soggy. Use a low or middle shelf, and preheat first.

For the record

Some brands draw a circle around the fan (fan oven + bottom heat). Same job: crispy bottoms.
