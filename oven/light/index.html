Oven, hob & microwave

Oven light

Also called: lamp, interior light, oven lamp, bulb

WTF it means

Switches on the light inside the oven, without any heating. A light bulb that means 'light'. Refreshing.

What to do about it

Use it to check on food without opening the door and letting the heat out. If it won't come on, the bulb has probably blown. Oven bulbs are special heat-proof ones, so buy the right type, and switch the oven off at the wall before changing it.

For the record

On most ovens the light also comes on by itself whenever you pick a cooking function.
