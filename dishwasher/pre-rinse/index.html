Dishwasher

Pre-rinse

Also called: pre rinse, prewash, rinse and hold, rinse only, soak

WTF it means

A short cold rinse with no detergent, to stop food drying onto the dishes when you won't run a full wash for a while.

What to do about it

Use it when the machine is half full and you won't run it until tomorrow. It stops smells and dried-on egg. Run a normal programme later.

For the record

No need to rinse plates under the tap before loading. Scraping off leftovers is enough, and modern detergents work better with a bit of food to grab onto.
