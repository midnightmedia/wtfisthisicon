Phones & gadgets

Do Not Disturb / Focus / Sleep (moon)

Also called: DND, Focus mode, quiet mode, silent notifications, sleep

WTF it means

On a phone the moon means Do Not Disturb (on iPhone, one of the Focus modes): calls and notifications arrive silently. The moon gets around, though. On a Windows power menu or some PC keyboards it means Sleep, but on newer Mac keyboards (2021 onwards) the moon key switches Do Not Disturb on and off. Next to a chat in Messages, it means you've muted that conversation.

What to do about it

Missing calls? Open Control Centre / Quick Settings and tap the moon (or Focus) to switch it off, and check it isn't scheduled to turn on automatically at night.

For the record

Many Android phones use a different Do Not Disturb icon: a circle with a horizontal line, like a no-entry sign.
