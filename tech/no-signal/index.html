Phones & gadgets

Circle with a line through it (no signal)

Also called: no signal, no service, no network, network unavailable

WTF it means

Samsung Galaxy phones show this when there's no mobile signal at all, so no calls, texts or mobile data until it comes back. iPhones say "No Service" in words instead.

What to do about it

Move somewhere with better reception, then switch flight mode on and off. Still nothing? Restart the phone and check the SIM is seated properly. If it lasts for hours, check your network's service status page.

For the record

Don't mix it up with a solid circle with a line across the middle. On Android, that one means Do Not Disturb.
