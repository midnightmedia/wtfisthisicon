Phones & gadgets

Rotation lock

Also called: portrait orientation lock, screen rotation lock, auto-rotate off

WTF it means

Your screen is locked the way it's facing, so it won't turn sideways when you rotate the phone or tablet.

What to do about it

Want videos to go full-screen sideways? Open Control Centre (iPhone) and tap the padlock-and-arrow button, or switch on Auto-rotate in Android's Quick Settings.

For the record

Great for reading in bed on your side. That's exactly why it exists.
