Phones & gadgets

Vibrate mode

Also called: vibration, buzz mode, silent with vibrate, meeting mode

WTF it means

The ringer is off, so the phone will buzz instead of ring for calls and notifications.

What to do about it

To get the sound back, press volume up, or tap the sound icon in Quick Settings / the volume panel until it shows a bell.

For the record

A bell with a line through it means fully silent (on some phones, not even a buzz).
