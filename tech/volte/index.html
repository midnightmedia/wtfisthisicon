VoLTE / HD voice

Also called: Voice over LTE, HD voice, Vo LTE, 4G calling

WTF it means

Your calls are going over the 4G network (Voice over LTE), which means clearer "HD" sound, faster connecting and still being able to use data during a call. A phone with a Wi-Fi symbol, or "Wi-Fi" next to the carrier name, means Wi-Fi calling.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. For once, a status icon with only good news. If calls keep failing, try switching VoLTE / 4G calling off and on in your mobile network settings.

For the record

As old 3G networks are switched off, phones that can't do VoLTE may lose the ability to make normal calls on some networks.
