Keyboard backlight

Also called: keyboard light, backlit keyboard, keyboard illumination, keyboard brightness

WTF it means

Turns the light behind the laptop keys on, off or brighter, for typing in the dark. It usually looks like a little keyboard or rectangle with rays shining out of the top.

What to do about it

Press it (often together with Fn) repeatedly to cycle through the light levels. On newer Macs you'll also find Keyboard Brightness in Control Centre.

For the record

Won't light up in a bright room? It may not be broken. Many laptops have a light sensor that turns the backlight off automatically in daylight.
