Phones & gadgets

Phone with a bent arrow (missed call)

Also called: missed call, missed call notification

WTF it means

You've missed a call. The arrow goes in and straight back out, because the call never got through to you.

What to do about it

Swipe down to see who it was, or open the recent calls in the Phone app. The icon goes once you've looked.

For the record

Android shows it with the other notification icons at the top left. iPhones don't use a status icon for this: you get a notification and a red badge on the Phone app instead.
