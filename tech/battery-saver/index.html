Phones & gadgets

Battery saver / Low Power Mode

Also called: Low Power Mode, power saving mode, battery saver, yellow battery

WTF it means

Battery-saving mode is on: the phone cuts background activity, dims things and slows down a little to make the charge last. On iPhone the battery icon turns yellow. Pixel and stock Android show a battery with a plus sign.

What to do about it

Leave it on if you're low. To switch it off, tap it in Control Centre / Quick Settings. iPhone switches it off by itself once it's charged past 80%.

For the record

A plus inside the battery doesn't mean charging. Charging is shown by a lightning bolt.
