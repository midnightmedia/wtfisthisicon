Phones & gadgets

Two rows of signal bars

Also called: Dual SIM, dual cell signals, eSIM, two SIMs, second line

WTF it means

Your phone has two phone lines (two SIMs, or a SIM and an eSIM), and each row shows the signal for one of them. On iPhone the top row is the line used for mobile data, and the bottom row is your other line.

What to do about it

Swipe down to Control Centre or Quick Settings to see which line is which. Don't need the second line? Turn it off in your SIM settings (Settings › Mobile Service on iPhone).

For the record

A leftover eSIM from a holiday data plan is a common reason this suddenly appears.
