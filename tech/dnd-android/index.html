Phones & gadgets

Circle with a line across the middle

Also called: Do Not Disturb, DND, Modes, Samsung Do Not Disturb, Pixel Do Not Disturb

WTF it means

Do Not Disturb is on, so most calls and notifications arrive silently. It's the Android version of the iPhone's moon.

What to do about it

Swipe down from the top and tap Do Not Disturb to switch it off. If it keeps coming back, check for a schedule (bedtime, for example) in your phone's Do Not Disturb or Modes settings.

For the record

You can let favourite contacts, alarms and repeat callers through, so not everything goes quiet.
