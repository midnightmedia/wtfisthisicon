Phones & gadgets

Airplane / flight mode

Also called: flight mode, aeroplane mode, offline mode

WTF it means

Flight mode is on: the phone's mobile signal is switched off, so no calls, texts or mobile data. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth usually switch off too, but you can turn them back on while staying in flight mode.

What to do about it

Didn't mean to? Swipe down to Control Centre / Quick Settings and tap the plane to turn it off.

For the record

Turning flight mode on and off again is a quick fix when your phone is stuck with no signal, as it forces it to search for the network afresh. It's the phone's version of turning it off and on again.
