Phones & gadgets

Ethernet / network port

Also called: LAN, RJ45, network cable, wired internet, Cat 5, Cat 6

WTF it means

The wired network (LAN) socket for an internet cable from your router. It's the chunky square socket with a clip notch, a bit wider than a phone socket, and still the most reliable way online.

What to do about it

Plug an Ethernet cable from the router in here for internet that's usually faster and more reliable than Wi-Fi. Great for TVs, consoles and desktop PCs.

For the record

The little lights next to the socket should blink when data flows. No lights usually means a dead cable, or the router end isn't plugged in. A phone cable will fit, but it won't work.
