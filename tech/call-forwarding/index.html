Phones & gadgets

Phone with an arrow (call forwarding)

Also called: call forwarding, call divert, diverted calls, forwarding

WTF it means

Call forwarding is on, so incoming calls are sent to another number instead of ringing your phone.

What to do about it

Missing calls? Turn it off in the Phone section of Settings on iPhone (Call Forwarding), or in the Phone app's settings on Android.

For the record

Scammers sometimes trick people into dialling a short code that forwards all their calls. If you didn't set it up, turn it off and tell your network.
