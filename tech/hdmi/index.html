Phones & gadgets

HDMI port

Also called: HDMI ARC, eARC, mini HDMI, micro HDMI, HDMI 2.1

WTF it means

HDMI carries picture and sound together from a laptop, console, streaming stick or set-top box to a TV, monitor or projector. One cable, both jobs.

What to do about it

Plug the cable in, then press Input or Source on the TV remote and choose the matching number (HDMI 1, HDMI 2…). No picture? Check you're on the right input before blaming the cable.

For the record

The port labelled ARC or eARC is the one for a soundbar. It lets the TV send its sound back down the same cable.
