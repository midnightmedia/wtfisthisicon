Phones & gadgets

Bluetooth

Also called: BT, wireless pairing, Bluetooth rune

WTF it means

Bluetooth: short-range wireless for headphones, speakers, watches, keyboards and cars. In a phone's status bar it means Bluetooth is on. Extra dots or a little battery next to it mean something is connected.

What to do about it

Switch it on or off in Control Centre / Quick Settings. On a gadget, holding the button with this symbol usually starts pairing. A fast-flashing light means it's ready to be found by your phone.

For the record

The logo merges two Viking runes, ᚼ (H) and ᛒ (B). They're the initials of Harald Bluetooth, a 10th-century Danish king famous for uniting tribes, just as Bluetooth unites gadgets. On a good day.
