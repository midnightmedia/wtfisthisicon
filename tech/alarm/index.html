Phones & gadgets

Alarm set

Also called: alarm clock, alarm on, wake-up alarm

WTF it means

An alarm is set on your phone. Nothing is ringing; one is just armed and ready.

What to do about it

Open the Clock app to see, change or switch off the alarm.

For the record

Phone alarms normally still ring even when the phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb. The alarm outranks them both, so you won't sleep through it.
