Phones & gadgets

Screen brightness (sun)

Also called: brightness up, brightness down, display brightness, auto-brightness

WTF it means

Screen brightness. Refreshingly, the sun really does mean light. A small sun usually means dimmer and a big sun brighter, and a sun with an "A" means automatic brightness.

What to do about it

On a laptop, press the key (sometimes together with Fn) to change brightness. On a phone, use the slider in Control Centre / Quick Settings.

For the record

Screen keeps dimming by itself? Auto-brightness or battery saver is probably on.
