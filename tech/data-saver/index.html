Phones & gadgets

Broken triangle with arrows inside (Data Saver)

Also called: Data Saver, data saving mode, Samsung Data Saver, background data restricted

WTF it means

Data Saver is on: to save your mobile data allowance, most apps can't use data in the background, and pictures and videos may load more slowly. Samsung Galaxy phones show it as this triangle.

What to do about it

Low on data this month? Leave it on. Apps not updating or messages arriving late? Turn it off in Quick Settings or Settings › Connections › Data usage.

For the record

Despite appearances, it isn't a recycling symbol. Nothing on your phone is being recycled.
