Phones & gadgets

Wi-Fi with an exclamation mark

Also called: no internet, Wi-Fi no internet, limited connectivity, connected, no internet

WTF it means

Your phone is connected to the Wi-Fi network, but that network can't reach the internet. The problem is usually the router or broadband, not your phone.

What to do about it

Restart the router: switch it off at the wall, wait 30 seconds, switch it back on. On public Wi-Fi, open a web page, as you may need to accept terms on a sign-in page first. In the meantime, turn Wi-Fi off to use mobile data.

For the record

If other devices on the same Wi-Fi are struggling too, that confirms it's the broadband and not your phone.
