Phones & gadgets

USB-C port

Also called: Type-C, USB Type-C, USB4, oval port

WTF it means

USB-C is the small, rounded, reversible plug used by most new phones, laptops and gadgets. The shape is the same everywhere, but what each port can do (charging, fast data, video, Thunderbolt) varies. Every port is a bit of a lucky dip.

What to do about it

Look at the little symbol beside the port: a lightning bolt with an arrowhead = Thunderbolt, a D-shaped "DP" icon = can send video to a monitor, a battery or plug icon = charging port, a number like 10/20/40 = speed in Gbps. If a monitor or dock doesn't work, try a different port and a better cable.

For the record

Cables matter too. Many cheap USB-C cables only charge and copy files slowly, and won't carry video at all.
