Phones & gadgets

Key in the status bar (VPN)

Also called: VPN, virtual private network, VPN key, VPN icon

WTF it means

You're connected through a VPN (virtual private network), so your internet traffic goes through another server first. Android phones show a key. iPhones show the letters VPN in a box.

What to do about it

If you set it up for work, streaming or privacy, all is well. If you didn't, look in your VPN settings to see which app added it, and remove anything you don't recognise.

For the record

Some antivirus, ad-blocking and work apps switch on a VPN without much fuss. It can also slow things down or confuse banking apps about where you are.
