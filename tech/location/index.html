Phones & gadgets

Location in use (arrow)

Also called: location services, GPS, navigation arrow, location arrow

WTF it means

An app is using your location (GPS). iPhone shows this slanted arrow; Android shows a map pin or a target. Either way, something knows where you are.

What to do about it

To see which app it is and stop it, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services (iPhone) or Settings › Location (Android). Then set apps to "While Using" or "Never".

For the record

In the iPhone Location Services list, a purple arrow means an app used your location recently, grey means within the last 24 hours, and a hollow outline means it may get your location under certain conditions.
