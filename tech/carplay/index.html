Phones & gadgets

Play button in a C (CarPlay)

Also called: CarPlay, Apple CarPlay, wireless CarPlay

WTF it means

Your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, so the car's screen is showing your phone's apps for maps, music and calls.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. Don't want it connecting to a particular car? Go to Settings › General › CarPlay, tap the car and choose Forget This Car.

For the record

Android phones have Android Auto, which does the same job.
