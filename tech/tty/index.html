Phone handset over a keyboard (TTY)

Also called: TTY, RTT, real-time text, text telephone, teletypewriter

WTF it means

TTY or RTT (real-time text) is switched on. It's an accessibility feature for people who are deaf, hard of hearing or speech-impaired, letting them type during a phone call.

What to do about it

Need it? All good. If not, switch it off in Settings › Accessibility › RTT/TTY on iPhone, or in the Phone app's accessibility settings on Android. It's sometimes switched on by accident.

For the record

TTY stands for teletypewriter, from the days when this meant a chunky typing machine plugged into a landline.
