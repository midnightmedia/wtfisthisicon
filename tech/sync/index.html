Phones & gadgets

Sync / refresh

Also called: syncing, refresh, reload, auto-sync, update

WTF it means

Syncing: the app or device is updating itself with the cloud or another device (email, photos, contacts, files). On a web page or in an app, the same idea means refresh / reload.

What to do about it

Usually just wait. If it spins forever, check your internet connection, then try closing and reopening the app.

For the record

A single curved arrow usually means reload or retry. Two arrows chasing each other usually means sync. They never do catch up.
