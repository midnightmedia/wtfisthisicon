Phones & gadgets

I and O on a switch

Also called: rocker switch, on/off switch, I/O switch, power switch

WTF it means

I means On and O means Off, from the binary digits 1 and 0. You'll find them on rocker switches on PCs, extension leads, kettles and power supplies.

What to do about it

Press the I side to switch on and the O side to switch off. On extension leads the switch usually lights up when it's on.

For the record

A switch marked 0, I and II (like on many hair dryers and fans) means off, low and high. II is not "on twice".
