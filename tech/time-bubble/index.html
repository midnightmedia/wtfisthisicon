Phones & gadgets

Coloured bubble around the time

Also called: green bubble around the time, red bubble around the time, blue bubble around the time, status bar pill, time highlighted

WTF it means

Something is still running in the background on your iPhone, and tapping the bubble takes you straight back to it. Sometimes the bubble holds a little picture instead of the time, like a phone or a record button. Green means a phone or FaceTime call, or someone using your Personal Hotspot. Red means your screen is being recorded. Blue means turn-by-turn directions are running or an app is using your location.

What to do about it

Tap the bubble to see what it is, then end the call, stop the recording or close the directions. Forgot you were screen recording? Tap the red bubble, then Stop.

For the record

iPhones with the Dynamic Island (the black pill at the top of the screen) usually show these as little animations in the island instead.
