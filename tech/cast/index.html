Phones & gadgets

Cast / Chromecast

Also called: Google Cast, Chromecast, cast to TV, Google TV

WTF it means

The Cast button: send a video or music from an app on your phone or laptop to a Chromecast, Google TV or cast-enabled speaker. When it's filled in, you're currently casting.

What to do about it

Tap it and choose your TV or speaker. Your phone and the TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

For the record

Once it's casting, your phone is just the remote: you can lock it or use other apps and the video keeps playing.
