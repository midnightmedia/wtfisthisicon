Phones & gadgets · Green light

Green dot at the top of the screen

Also called: camera indicator, privacy indicator, camera in use, green dot iPhone, green dot Android, green light on my phone

WTF it means

An app is using your camera right now (on Android it can also mean the microphone). It's a privacy warning built into iPhone and Android, so nothing can film you without the dot showing up.

What to do about it

On a video call or taking photos? That's the reason. If not, swipe down from the top right (Control Centre on iPhone, Quick Settings on Android) to see which app it is, then turn off its camera access in your Privacy settings.

For the record

On iPhone, green means the camera (and maybe the microphone along with it), and orange means the microphone only. Android uses green for both.
