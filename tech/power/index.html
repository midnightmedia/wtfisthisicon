Phones & gadgets

Power / standby button

Also called: power symbol, standby, on/off button, IEC 5009, ⏻

WTF it means

It's the power button. Strictly, the broken circle with a line poking through means "standby", so pressing it often sends the device to sleep rather than cutting the power completely.

What to do about it

Press once to switch on or wake up. To turn off properly, hold it for a few seconds or use the on-screen menu. Gadget frozen? Holding it for around 10 seconds usually forces it off.

For the record

It's a mash-up of binary 1 (on) and 0 (off). Very tidy, if you happen to think in binary. The rest of the family: a plain line | means On, a circle O means Off, a line inside a complete circle means an on/off toggle, and a crescent moon means Sleep.
