Phones & gadgets

Hearing loop / T-coil

Also called: hearing loop, induction loop, T-coil, telecoil, hearing aid compatible, HAC, T setting

WTF it means

A hearing loop is installed. Sound from the counter, venue, lift or device goes straight to hearing aids switched to T (telecoil), cutting out background noise.

What to do about it

If you wear hearing aids with a telecoil, switch them to the T or "loop" programme. If you don't wear hearing aids, you can ignore it.

For the record

On a phone box, a T rating (like T3 or T4) means it works with a hearing aid's telecoil, and higher is better. Without the T, the same crossed-out ear is the general symbol for deaf or hard-of-hearing access.
