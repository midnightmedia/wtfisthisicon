Phones & gadgets

AirPlay / screen mirroring

Also called: AirPlay, screen mirroring, stream to TV, Apple TV

WTF it means

Apple's AirPlay: beam the video, music or whole screen from your iPhone, iPad or Mac to an Apple TV, AirPlay TV or speaker. A version with circles instead of a rectangle means audio-only AirPlay.

What to do about it

Tap it and choose the TV or speaker. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi. Sound suddenly vanished from your phone? It may still be playing on another device, possibly to an empty room. Tap here and choose your phone.

For the record

Screen Mirroring in iPhone's Control Centre is the icon of two overlapping rectangles.
