Phones & gadgets

Battery with a lightning bolt

Also called: charging icon, battery charging, optimised battery charging, charging paused, charge limit

WTF it means

Your phone is charging. On iPhone the battery turns green while it charges. If it's grey instead, charging has been paused on purpose.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. Stuck at 80%? That's Optimised Battery Charging or a charge limit looking after the battery, and it'll top up later (tap the notification to charge to 100% now). It also pauses if the phone gets too warm. Not charging at all? Try another cable, plug and socket.

For the record

A plus sign in the battery isn't charging, that's battery saver. On iPhone, a yellow battery means Low Power Mode is on, and a red one means it's below 20%.
