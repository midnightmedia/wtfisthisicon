Phones & gadgets

Satellite icon

Also called: Emergency SOS via satellite, satellite mode, SAT, Messages via satellite, satellite SOS

WTF it means

Your phone has no mobile signal, but it can reach a satellite instead. On iPhone 14 and later that means emergency texts, Find My and, in some countries, Messages and Roadside Assistance still work, as long as you're outside with a clear view of the sky.

What to do about it

In an emergency, call 999 or 112 as normal. If the call can't connect, the iPhone offers to text emergency services by satellite. Otherwise, carry on until you're back in signal.

For the record

"SAT" in place of the signal bars is slightly different: your mobile network itself is using a satellite to keep you connected. Some newer Android phones have satellite SOS too.
