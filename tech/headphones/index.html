Phones & gadgets

Headphone / headset jack

Also called: 3.5mm jack, audio jack, aux, combo jack, mic jack, line out

WTF it means

Marks the 3.5mm socket for wired headphones, which plenty of phones have quietly abolished. A headset icon (headphones with a little microphone) means it's a combo jack that also takes a microphone.

What to do about it

Plug headphones in here. On computers with separate sockets, the green one is for headphones/speakers and the pink one (with a microphone icon) is for a microphone. A light-blue socket is line-in.

For the record

Headset mic not working in a combo jack? The plug may need four contacts (three black rings). For computers with separate sockets, you need a cheap splitter.
