Phones & gadgets · Amber light

Orange dot at the top of the screen

Also called: microphone indicator, privacy indicator, mic in use, orange dot iPhone, recording indicator

WTF it means

An app is using your iPhone's microphone right now. It's a privacy warning, so nothing can listen in without the dot appearing.

What to do about it

On a call, talking to Siri, dictating or recording a voice note? That's the reason. If not, open Control Centre: the app's name shows at the top. You can switch off its microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

For the record

With the accessibility setting Differentiate Without Colour switched on, the orange dot becomes an orange square, so it's easier to tell apart from the green one.
