Phones & gadgets

Phone with Wi-Fi waves

Also called: Wi-Fi calling, VoWiFi, WiFi call, Wi-Fi Call

WTF it means

Wi-Fi calling is switched on: when mobile signal is weak, calls and texts go over Wi-Fi instead. iPhones don't use a picture for it, just the word "Wi-Fi" next to your network's name.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's handy in homes with poor reception indoors. You can switch it on or off in your phone's call or mobile network settings.

For the record

VoLTE or HD next to the signal bars is a different thing: that means calls are going over 4G.
