Phones & gadgets

Wi-Fi

Also called: WiFi, wireless internet, WLAN, Wi-Fi 6, Wi-Fi 7

WTF it means

You're connected to a Wi-Fi network, and the number of filled arcs shows the signal strength. An ! or ✕ next to it means connected to Wi-Fi but no internet. A padlock (in the network list) means it needs a password. A 6, 6E or 7 means a newer, faster Wi-Fi standard.

What to do about it

No internet with an !? Restart your router (unplug it for 30 seconds). Weak signal? Move closer to the router or away from thick walls and microwaves.

For the record

"Wi-Fi" doesn't actually stand for "wireless fidelity". A branding agency invented the catchy name, and the backronym came later.
