All symbols

Phone and gadget symbols

Ports, status bars & buttons. 30 of them, decoded.

Power / standby button

It's the power button.

I and O on a switch

I means On and O means Off, from the binary digits 1 and 0.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth: short-range wireless for headphones, speakers, watches, keyboards and cars.

USB symbol

Marks a USB port or cable: the everyday plug for charging and connecting mice, keyboards, memory sticks and drives.

USB-C port

USB-C is the small, rounded, reversible plug used by most new phones, laptops and gadgets.

Thunderbolt

This port supports Thunderbolt, a super-fast connection for docks, multiple monitors, fast external drives and graphics boxes.

HDMI port

HDMI carries picture and sound together from a laptop, console, streaming stick or set-top box to a TV, monitor or projector.

Ethernet / network port

The wired network (LAN) socket for an internet cable from your router.

Headphone / headset jack

Marks the 3.5mm socket for wired headphones, which plenty of phones have quietly abolished.

Wi-Fi

You're connected to a Wi-Fi network, and the number of filled arcs shows the signal strength.

Contactless / NFC

Contactless: tap a card, phone or watch here to pay or scan.

Airplane / flight mode

Flight mode is on: the phone's mobile signal is switched off, so no calls, texts or mobile data.

Do Not Disturb / Focus / Sleep (moon)

On a phone the moon means Do Not Disturb (on iPhone, one of the Focus modes): calls and notifications arrive silently.

Personal hotspot / tethering

Your phone is sharing its mobile data with other devices over Wi-Fi, acting as a mini router.

Data roaming (R)

Your phone is connected to a network that isn't your own.

Network letters (E, 3G, H+, 4G, LTE, 5G)

Shows which kind of mobile data connection you've got.

VoLTE / HD voice

Your calls are going over the 4G network (Voice over LTE), which means clearer "HD" sound, faster connecting and still being able to use data during a call.

Location in use (arrow)

An app is using your location (GPS).

Battery saver / Low Power Mode

Battery-saving mode is on: the phone cuts background activity, dims things and slows down a little to make the charge last.

AirPlay / screen mirroring

Apple's AirPlay: beam the video, music or whole screen from your iPhone, iPad or Mac to an Apple TV, AirPlay TV or speaker.

Cast / Chromecast

The Cast button: send a video or music from an app on your phone or laptop to a Chromecast, Google TV or cast-enabled speaker.

Eject

Eject: open a disc tray, or (on a computer) safely disconnect a drive or memory stick.

Screen brightness (sun)

Screen brightness.

Keyboard backlight

Turns the light behind the laptop keys on, off or brighter, for typing in the dark.

Alarm set

An alarm is set on your phone.

Vibrate mode

The ringer is off, so the phone will buzz instead of ring for calls and notifications.

Rotation lock

Your screen is locked the way it's facing, so it won't turn sideways when you rotate the phone or tablet.

Sync / refresh

Syncing: the app or device is updating itself with the cloud or another device (email, photos, contacts, files).

Hearing loop / T-coil

A hearing loop is installed.

SOS in the status bar

Your phone can't connect to your own network, but it can still make emergency calls using any other network that's available.
