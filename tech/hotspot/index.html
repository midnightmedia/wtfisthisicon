Phones & gadgets

Personal hotspot / tethering

Also called: mobile hotspot, tethering, personal hotspot, portable hotspot

WTF it means

Your phone is sharing its mobile data with other devices over Wi-Fi, acting as a mini router. Android shows a dot with arcs around it. iPhone uses a chain-link icon, plus a coloured bubble around the clock when someone is connected.

What to do about it

Don't need it? Turn it off in Quick Settings / Control Centre. It uses your data allowance and drains the battery quickly.

For the record

Some phone plans limit or charge extra for hotspot data, even if your normal data is "unlimited".
