Phones & gadgets

Data roaming (R)

Also called: roaming, R icon, roaming indicator

WTF it means

Your phone is connected to a network that isn't your own. Usually that's because you're abroad, and sometimes because your provider borrows another network's masts.

What to do about it

Check your plan's roaming rules before using lots of data. To avoid surprise bills, turn off Data Roaming in your mobile network settings and use Wi-Fi instead.

For the record

Seeing R at home? You haven't wandered abroad by mistake. Some providers piggyback on partner masts in rural areas. It's normally free, but worth checking with them.
