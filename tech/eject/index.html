Phones & gadgets

Eject

Also called: eject button, open tray, safely remove

WTF it means

Eject: open a disc tray, or (on a computer) safely disconnect a drive or memory stick. Same triangle, two jobs, one of them mostly retired.

What to do about it

Before pulling out a USB stick or memory card on a Mac, click the eject symbol next to it in Finder (or "Safely Remove" on Windows) so files finish saving and don't get corrupted.

For the record

You'll see the same triangle-on-a-bar on old video and DVD remotes, where it opens the tray.
