Phones & gadgets

Network letters (E, 3G, H+, 4G, LTE, 5G)

Also called: LTE, 4G, 4G+, LTE+, 5G, 5G+, 5G UW, H+, E, EDGE, G, GPRS, 3G

WTF it means

Shows which kind of mobile data connection you've got. From slowest to fastest: G (GPRS) and E (EDGE) are ancient 2G and very slow; 3G and H/H+ are okay; 4G/LTE is fast; 4G+/LTE+ combines several channels; 5G is the newest.

What to do about it

See E or G? The internet will crawl, so move somewhere with better signal or switch to Wi-Fi. Never see 5G? Your phone, plan or area may not support it yet.

For the record

In the US, AT&T's old "5G E" icon was actually souped-up 4G with a marketing makeover.
