Phones & gadgets

SOS in the status bar

Also called: SOS only, emergency calls only, no service

WTF it means

Your phone can't connect to your own network, but it can still make emergency calls using any other network that's available. It isn't an alarm, and nothing has been sent.

What to do about it

Move somewhere with better signal, toggle flight mode on and off, or restart the phone. If it lasts, check your SIM and account with your provider. Emergency calls still work.

For the record

Android shows "Emergency calls only" in the same situation. Newer iPhones may also show a satellite icon where emergency texts by satellite are possible.
