Phones & gadgets

USB symbol

Also called: USB trident, USB-A, universal serial bus, SS USB, SuperSpeed

WTF it means

Marks a USB port or cable: the everyday plug for charging and connecting mice, keyboards, memory sticks and drives.

What to do about it

Plug USB things in here. "SS" (SuperSpeed) next to it means the port is the faster USB 3 kind, so use it for external drives. A battery or lightning-bolt icon beside a port means it can charge your phone, sometimes even while the computer is asleep.

For the record

The circle, square and triangle on the branches are said to show that USB connects all kinds of devices. A blue plastic tongue inside a rectangular USB port usually means USB 3.
