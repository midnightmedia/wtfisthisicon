Phones & gadgets

Up and down arrows (mobile data)

Also called: mobile data, data arrows, data activity, upload and download arrows, cellular data

WTF it means

Mobile data is switched on. On Samsung and other Android phones, little arrows under the 4G or 5G letters light up when data is being downloaded (down arrow) or uploaded (up arrow).

What to do about it

Nothing to do. Worried about your data allowance? Connect to Wi-Fi, or switch mobile data off in Quick Settings.

For the record

The same arrows next to the Wi-Fi symbol show data moving over Wi-Fi instead.
