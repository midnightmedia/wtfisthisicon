Electric & hybrid cars · Green light

Ready-to-drive light (car with arrows)

Also called: ready to drive indicator, ready indicator, power on, READY

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The car is switched on and ready to drive: the electric-car equivalent of the engine running. Nissan (the Leaf, for example) and some others use this symbol instead of the word READY, for reasons best known to themselves.

What to do about it

Select D (or R) and drive. If it won't come on, press the brake firmly while pressing the power button, and make sure the charging cable is unplugged.

For the record

The car is silent but live: press the accelerator in D and it will move. Always select P and switch off before you get out.
