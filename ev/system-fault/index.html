Electric & hybrid cars · Red light

Electric / hybrid system fault

Also called: EV system warning, hybrid system warning, electric drive fault, high-voltage system fault, powertrain fault, check EV system

Deal with it now

WTF it means

The car has found a problem in its electric drive system: the high-voltage battery, the motor, the inverter or the computer that runs them. It may cut power, or shut the drive system down to protect itself.

What to do about it

Pull over somewhere safe as soon as you can, switch the car off, wait a minute and restart it. If the light comes back, or the car won't go back into READY, don't drive on. Call the manufacturer's breakdown assistance. Never touch or try to fix the high-voltage parts (the bright orange cables) yourself.

For the record

Brands differ. Toyota and Lexus hybrids use a car with ! as their hybrid system warning. Some makers show it in amber for less serious faults, or use a car with a spanner for 'service needed'. Your handbook has the exact meaning for your car.
