Electric & hybrid cars · Green light

Battery charge mode (plug-in hybrid)

Also called: CHARGE mode, battery charge, e-charge, charge mode, Battery Control, battery hold, save mode

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

Plug-in hybrids only: the petrol engine is running partly to recharge the battery as you drive. That saves electric range for later (say, for a city centre or low-emission zone).

What to do about it

Use it only when you really need electric range later. Charging the battery with the engine burns noticeably more fuel than plugging in. Switch back to the normal hybrid/auto mode with the drive-mode button.

For the record

A similar 'Hold' or 'Save' mode just keeps the battery at its current level without adding charge. Naturally, the names vary by brand: CHARGE (Mitsubishi), Battery Control (BMW), Battery Charge (VW group).
