Electric & hybrid cars · Red light

Battery overheating

Also called: battery temperature warning, high-voltage battery too hot, EV battery overheat, hybrid system overheat, traction battery temperature

Deal with it now

WTF it means

The big drive battery is getting too hot, for example after several rapid charges in a row, hard driving on a hot day, or a fault in the battery's cooling system. The car will usually limit power and charging speed to protect it.

What to do about it

Ease off, stop somewhere safe and let it cool down. If you're rapid charging, end the session. If the light won't go out, get it checked before driving far. If you see smoke, smell burning, or hear hissing or popping from underneath, get everyone out, move well away and call the emergency services.

For the record

A battery with a blue snowflake is the opposite problem: the battery is too cold. That's normal in winter and nothing to worry about.
