Electric & hybrid cars · Amber light

Pedestrian warning sound off

Also called: AVAS off, VESS off, VSP off, vehicle proximity notification off, pedestrian alert off, acoustic vehicle alerting system, sound generator off

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The low-speed pedestrian warning sound is switched off, or isn't working. Electric cars and hybrids play an artificial hum at low speeds so pedestrians, cyclists and blind or partially sighted people can hear them coming.

What to do about it

If your car has an off button (usually marked with the same symbol), press it to turn the sound back on. Then leave it on, especially in car parks and busy streets. If you didn't switch it off, the speaker or system has a fault. Get it fixed, as the sound is a legal requirement in many countries.

For the record

The hum only plays at low speed: up to about 20 km/h (12 mph) in the EU and 30 km/h (19 mph) in the US. Above that, tyre noise is loud enough. Many newer cars no longer have an off switch at all, so on those this light always means a fault.
