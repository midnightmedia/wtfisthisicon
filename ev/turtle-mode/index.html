Electric & hybrid cars · Amber light

Turtle (reduced power mode)

Also called: power limited, limited power, power down warning, reduced power, turtle light, tortoise light, power limitation indicator, limp mode

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The car has deliberately cut its power to protect the high-voltage battery or motor, so you'll feel weak acceleration and a lower top speed. The usual reasons: the battery is almost empty, it's very hot or very cold, or there's a fault in the electric drive system.

What to do about it

Get out of fast traffic and drive gently. If the battery is nearly flat, head straight for the nearest charger: you may only have a few miles left before the car stops. If the battery isn't low, let the car cool down (or warm up). If the turtle keeps coming back, get it checked.

For the record

The Nissan Leaf made the turtle famous. Hyundai and Kia call theirs the 'Power Down' light. Plenty of other EVs and hybrids skip the wildlife and just show a message such as 'Power limited'.
