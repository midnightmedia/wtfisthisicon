Electric & hybrid cars · Amber light

Low drive battery (low charge)

Also called: low battery charge warning, low state of charge, low SoC, range warning, high-voltage battery low, traction battery low, EV low fuel

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The big drive battery (the one that powers the motor) is running low. It's the EV version of the low fuel light, and usually comes on somewhere around 10–20% charge, depending on the car.

What to do about it

Head for a charger now. Turn down the heating or air con and drive gently to stretch the range. Push on regardless and the car will eventually limit power (hello, turtle light) before it stops.

For the record

This is NOT the red 'box with + and −' battery light. That one is about the small 12-volt battery, which runs the lights, locks and computers. EVs and hybrids have both: a huge traction battery for driving and an ordinary 12V battery to 'boot up' the car. A flat 12V battery can stop an EV starting even with a full traction battery. It can be jump-started like any other car's.
