Electric and hybrid car symbols

Turtles, plugs and READY lights. 14 of them, decoded.

Red = stop safely & check now Amber = get it checked soon Green / blue = a system is on, all fine

Electric / hybrid system fault

The car has found a problem in its electric drive system: the high-voltage battery, the motor, the inverter or the computer that runs them.

Deal with it now

Battery overheating

The big drive battery is getting too hot, for example after several rapid charges in a row, hard driving on a hot day, or a fault in the battery's cooling system.

Deal with it now

Turtle (reduced power mode)

The car has deliberately cut its power to protect the high-voltage battery or motor, so you'll feel weak acceleration and a lower top speed.

Sort it soon

Low drive battery (low charge)

The big drive battery (the one that powers the motor) is running low.

Sort it soon

High-voltage battery warning

The car has spotted a problem with the big drive battery or the system that manages it (for example a sensor, cell-balance or cooling fault).

Sort it soon

Charging cable connected

A charging cable is still plugged into the car.

Relax, it's fine

Pedestrian warning sound off

The low-speed pedestrian warning sound is switched off, or isn't working.

Relax, it's fine

READY light

The car is switched on and ready to drive.

Relax, it's fine

Ready-to-drive light (car with arrows)

The car is switched on and ready to drive: the electric-car equivalent of the engine running.

Relax, it's fine

Charging in progress

The high-voltage battery is charging.

Relax, it's fine

Cold battery (snowflake)

The high-voltage battery is cold.

Relax, it's fine

EV mode (electric-only driving)

On a hybrid or plug-in hybrid, the car is running on electricity alone, with the petrol engine off.

Relax, it's fine

Battery charge mode (plug-in hybrid)

Plug-in hybrids only: the petrol engine is running partly to recharge the battery as you drive.

Relax, it's fine

B mode (regenerative braking)

B makes the car slow down more strongly when you lift off the accelerator.

Relax, it's fine
