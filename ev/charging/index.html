Electric & hybrid cars · Green light

Charging in progress

Also called: charging indicator, battery charging, plugged in and charging, charge status, charge port light

Relax, it's fine

WTF it means

The high-voltage battery is charging. You'll usually see the percentage or time remaining on the dash or screen too, plus a light by the charge port (often pulsing while charging, solid when full).

What to do about it

Nothing to do. When you're finished, stop the charge (in the app, on the charger, or by unlocking the car), unplug, and close the charge flap.

For the record

Charge-port light colours vary by brand. On a Tesla, for example: flashing green = charging, solid green = done, amber = plug not fully pushed in, red = fault.
