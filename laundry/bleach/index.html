Clothing labels

Any bleach allowed

Also called: bleach ok, chlorine bleach allowed, bleach when needed

WTF it means

An empty triangle means any bleach can be used if needed, chlorine or oxygen (non-chlorine). For a shape that looks like a warning sign, it's surprisingly relaxed.

What to do about it

Bleach is optional. Follow the instructions on the bottle and always dilute chlorine bleach.

For the record

Older labels sometimes show 'Cl' inside the triangle. That also means chlorine bleach is OK.
