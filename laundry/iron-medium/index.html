Clothing labels

Iron on medium heat

Also called: warm iron, medium iron, 150 °C

WTF it means

Iron at a medium temperature, max 150 °C (300 °F). Two dots is the middle setting, typical for polyester, wool, silk and blends.

What to do about it

Set the iron to two dots. For wool and silk, iron inside out or use a pressing cloth to avoid shiny patches.

For the record

Just match the dots on the label to the ones on your iron's dial.
