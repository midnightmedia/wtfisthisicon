Clothing labels

Machine wash

Also called: washable, machine wash normal, washtub

WTF it means

The washtub means it can go in the washing machine. On US labels, an empty tub means a normal machine wash at any temperature that suits the fabric.

What to do about it

Use a normal cycle. If in doubt, 30–40 °C is the safe middle ground.

For the record

This is the base shape for every washing symbol. A number (Europe) or dots (US) inside give the maximum temperature, and lines underneath ask for a gentler cycle.
