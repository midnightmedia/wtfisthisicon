Clothing labels

Do not wash

Also called: not washable, no washing

WTF it means

Don't wash it at all, not in a machine and not by hand. Water will damage it: shrinking, staining or losing its shape.

What to do about it

Check the other symbols. Usually it needs professional dry cleaning (a circle). Spot-clean small marks carefully if you must.

For the record

A big X through any care symbol simply means 'don't do this'. Finally, one that needs no translation.
