Clothing labels

Tumble dry, low heat

Also called: low heat, gentle tumble dry, delicate dry

WTF it means

Tumble drying is fine, but only on low heat. The standard allows an exhaust temperature of max 60 °C.

What to do about it

Choose the low-heat, delicate or gentle setting on your dryer.

For the record

Too much heat is the main reason clothes shrink in the dryer. If yours has no low setting, air-dry instead.
