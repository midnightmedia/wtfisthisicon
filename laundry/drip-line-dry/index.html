Clothing labels

Drip line dry (two vertical lines)

Also called: drip dry, hang dripping wet, no spin

WTF it means

Hang it up dripping wet, without spinning or wringing. This is the European (ISO) drip-dry symbol.

What to do about it

Use the no-spin option or take it out before the spin, then hang it somewhere the drips don't matter.

For the record

Spinning would crease or stretch the fabric, while drip-drying lets the weight of the water pull the creases out. US labels use three lines for this; Europe makes do with two.
