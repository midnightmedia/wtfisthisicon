Clothing care label symbols

The hieroglyphs on the care tag. 34 of them, decoded.

Machine wash

The washtub means it can go in the washing machine.

Wash at 30 °C

Machine wash at no more than 30 °C (86 °F).

Wash at 40 °C

Machine wash at no more than 40 °C (104 °F).

Wash at 60 °C

Machine wash at up to 60 °C (140 °F).

Wash at 95 °C

It can be washed at up to 95 °C (around 200 °F), which is practically boiling.

Gentle wash (one line underneath)

One line under the tub means a gentler machine cycle, with less agitation and a slower spin, like the 'synthetics', 'easy care' or 'permanent press' programme.

Very gentle wash (two lines underneath)

Two lines under the tub mean a very gentle machine wash, with minimal agitation and a low spin, as on a 'wool', 'delicates' or 'silk' programme.

Hand wash only

Wash by hand only, in water no hotter than 40 °C (104 °F).

Do not wash

Don't wash it at all, not in a machine and not by hand.

Any bleach allowed

An empty triangle means any bleach can be used if needed, chlorine or oxygen (non-chlorine).

Non-chlorine bleach only

Only oxygen-based, non-chlorine bleach (the 'colour-safe' kind) is allowed.

Do not bleach

Don't use any bleach at all, chlorine or oxygen.

Tumble dry

A circle inside a square means it can go in the tumble dryer.

Tumble dry, low heat

Tumble drying is fine, but only on low heat.

Tumble dry, normal heat

Tumble drying at normal (higher) heat is fine.

Tumble dry, no heat

You can tumble it, but with unheated air only (the 'air fluff' or 'no heat' setting).

Do not tumble dry

Keep it out of the tumble dryer.

Line dry

Hang it up to dry on a clothesline, airer or hanger.

Line dry (vertical line)

Hang it up to dry after spinning, on a line or a hanger.

Drip dry

Hang it up soaking wet, straight out of the wash.

Drip line dry (two vertical lines)

Hang it up dripping wet, without spinning or wringing.

Dry flat

Lay it flat to dry, on a towel or a mesh drying rack, instead of hanging it.

Dry in the shade

Dry it out of direct sunlight, which can fade colours or yellow some fabrics.

Iron on low heat

Iron at a low temperature, max 110 °C (230 °F).

Iron on medium heat

Iron at a medium temperature, max 150 °C (300 °F).

Iron on high heat

Iron at a high temperature, max 200 °C (390 °F).

Do not iron

Don't iron it.

Do not steam

Iron on a low setting (that's the single dot), and without steam.

Dry clean

A plain circle means it can be professionally dry cleaned (US labels).

Dry clean (P)

Professional dry cleaning with tetrachloroethylene ('perc') or any of the milder hydrocarbon solvents is fine.

Dry clean (F)

One for the professionals: dry cleaning with hydrocarbon (petroleum-based) solvents only.

Professional wet clean (W)

It can be professionally wet cleaned: a specialist water-based process with careful detergents and controlled drying.

Do not dry clean

Don't dry clean it.

Do not wring

Don't twist or wring it to get the water out.
