Clothing labels

Non-chlorine bleach only

Also called: oxygen bleach only, colour-safe bleach, no chlorine bleach

WTF it means

Only oxygen-based, non-chlorine bleach (the 'colour-safe' kind) is allowed. Chlorine bleach would damage or discolour it.

What to do about it

If you need a stain remover, use a colour-safe (oxygen) one, and keep household chlorine bleach well away.

For the record

Many everyday detergents already contain oxygen bleach, so washing as normal is fine.
