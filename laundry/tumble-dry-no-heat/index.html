Clothing labels

Tumble dry, no heat

Also called: air fluff, air only, no heat, cool tumble

WTF it means

You can tumble it, but with unheated air only (the 'air fluff' or 'no heat' setting). It's a US-style symbol, and not the most obvious one.

What to do about it

Use the air-only, no-heat or refresh setting, or just let it air-dry.

For the record

The air-only setting is handy for shaking dust out of curtains, cushions and pillows without any heat.
