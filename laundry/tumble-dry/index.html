Clothing labels

Tumble dry

Also called: dryer safe, tumble dry normal, machine dry

WTF it means

A circle inside a square means it can go in the tumble dryer. With no dots, any normal heat setting is fine.

What to do about it

Dry on a normal setting and take it out promptly to avoid creases.

For the record

Dots inside set the heat: one dot = low, two dots = normal, and US labels add three dots for high. A filled-in circle means no heat at all.
