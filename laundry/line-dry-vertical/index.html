Clothing labels

Line dry (vertical line)

Also called: line drying, hang to dry, hang up

WTF it means

Hang it up to dry after spinning, on a line or a hanger. This is the European (ISO) line-drying symbol.

What to do about it

Hang it up and let it air-dry. Keep it out of the tumble dryer unless another symbol allows it.

For the record

One vertical line = hang to dry. Two = hang it up dripping wet (no spin). Add two diagonal lines in the corner and it means 'in the shade'. A lot of meaning for a few lines.
