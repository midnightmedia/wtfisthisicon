Clothing labels

Iron on low heat

Also called: cool iron, low iron, 110 °C

WTF it means

Iron at a low temperature, max 110 °C (230 °F). One dot means go gently: it's typical for delicate synthetics like acrylic, nylon or acetate.

What to do about it

Set the iron to its one-dot setting and skip the steam. At this setting, steam can cause permanent damage.

For the record

Most irons have the same dots printed on the dial, so just match them up. A rare bit of joined-up thinking.
