Clothing labels

Very gentle wash (two lines underneath)

Also called: delicate cycle, wool cycle, delicates, very mild wash

WTF it means

Two lines under the tub mean a very gentle machine wash, with minimal agitation and a low spin, as on a 'wool', 'delicates' or 'silk' programme. Stick to the temperature in the tub.

What to do about it

Use the wool or delicates programme, wash a small load, and consider a mesh laundry bag.

For the record

Two lines is as gentle as machine washing gets. One step further is the hand-wash symbol: a hand in the tub.
