Clothing labels

Do not tumble dry

Also called: no dryer, no tumble dry, air dry only

WTF it means

Keep it out of the tumble dryer. The heat and tumbling could shrink, melt, pill or misshape it, and none of those come out in the wash.

What to do about it

Air-dry instead. One of the natural-drying symbols (a square with lines) should show you how.

For the record

You'll often see it on wool, very stretchy clothes, printed T-shirts and anything with rubbery or plastic parts.
