Clothing labels

Gentle wash (one line underneath)

Also called: permanent press, synthetics cycle, easy care, mild wash

WTF it means

One line under the tub means a gentler machine cycle, with less agitation and a slower spin, like the 'synthetics', 'easy care' or 'permanent press' programme. The number still gives the maximum temperature.

What to do about it

Choose the synthetics, easy-care or permanent press programme at or below the temperature shown, and don't overload the drum.

For the record

Underlining usually means 'important'. Here it means 'calm down': no line = normal, one = gentle, two = very gentle. It works the same under dry-cleaning circles (and under tumble-dry symbols on US labels).
