Clothing labels

Dry flat

Also called: lay flat to dry, flat drying

WTF it means

Lay it flat to dry, on a towel or a mesh drying rack, instead of hanging it.

What to do about it

Gently reshape it while damp and lay it flat away from direct heat. Turn it over once so it dries evenly.

For the record

Typical for knitted wool and cashmere: hang a wet jumper and the water's weight stretches it into a dress. Two horizontal lines (European labels) means lay it flat while still dripping wet.
