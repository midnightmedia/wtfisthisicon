Clothing labels

Do not bleach

Also called: no bleach

WTF it means

Don't use any bleach at all, chlorine or oxygen.

What to do about it

Skip bleach and bleach-based stain removers. Use a 'colour' detergent, since powders for whites often contain oxygen bleach.

For the record

US labels sometimes draw this as a solid black triangle with the cross. Same meaning, more ink.
