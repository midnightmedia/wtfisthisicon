Clothing labels

Iron on high heat

Also called: hot iron, high iron, 200 °C

WTF it means

Iron at a high temperature, max 200 °C (390 °F). Typical for cotton and linen, which can take it.

What to do about it

Set the iron to three dots and use steam freely. Linen irons best while slightly damp.

For the record

On US labels, an iron with no dots at all means any temperature is OK.
