Clothing labels

Wash at 95 °C

Also called: boil wash, very hot wash, 95 degrees, 200 °F

WTF it means

It can be washed at up to 95 °C (around 200 °F), which is practically boiling. Only the toughest white cottons and linens are up to it, such as tea towels, cloths and some bedding.

What to do about it

Use the hottest cotton cycle for really grubby or hygiene-critical items. For everyday dirt, 60 °C is plenty.

For the record

On US labels, six dots in the tub means the same as 95 (1 dot = 30, 2 = 40, 3 = 50, 4 = 60, 5 = 70, 6 = 95 °C).
