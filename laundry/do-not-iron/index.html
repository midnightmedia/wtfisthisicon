Clothing labels

Do not iron

Also called: no ironing, no iron

WTF it means

Don't iron it. Heat would melt, glaze or flatten the fabric or a print. The European version also rules out steaming.

What to do about it

Hang it up straight after washing so the creases drop out on their own. As instructions go, it's one of the nicer ones.

For the record

Often found on clothes with plastic prints, sequins, velvet or heat-sensitive synthetics.
