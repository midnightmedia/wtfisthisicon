Clothing labels

Dry clean

Also called: dry cleaning, professional cleaning, dry clean only

WTF it means

A plain circle means it can be professionally dry cleaned (US labels). Dry cleaning uses solvents instead of water.

What to do about it

Take it to a dry cleaner. This isn't a home job, and 'dry clean' doesn't mean 'dry it'.

For the record

Circles are for professional cleaners, and a letter inside (P, F or W) tells them which process to use. A circle inside a square is something else entirely: tumble drying.
