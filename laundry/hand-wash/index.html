Clothing labels

Hand wash only

Also called: hand wash, wash by hand, no machine wash

WTF it means

Wash by hand only, in water no hotter than 40 °C (104 °F). Keep it out of a normal machine cycle.

What to do about it

Use lukewarm water and a little gentle detergent. Squeeze rather than rub or wring, rinse, then press the water out in a towel.

For the record

Many modern machines have a hand-wash or wool programme that's gentle enough. Check your machine's manual before trusting it with your favourite jumper.
