Clothing labels

Do not steam

Also called: no steam, iron without steam, dry iron only

WTF it means

Iron on a low setting (that's the single dot), and without steam. The crossed-out lines under the iron are the steam, and steam could leave water marks or damage the fabric.

What to do about it

Switch off the steam (or empty the water tank) and dry-iron at the temperature the label allows.

For the record

Silk and some synthetics get water spots from steam, and they're hard to get out again.
