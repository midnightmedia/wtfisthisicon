Clothing labels

Do not wring

Also called: no wringing, do not twist

WTF it means

Don't twist or wring it to get the water out. That stretches and distorts the fibres.

What to do about it

Gently squeeze the water out, or roll it up in a towel like a Swiss roll and press. Then dry it as the label says.

For the record

Mostly a US-label symbol, usually on knits, wool and delicate fabrics.
