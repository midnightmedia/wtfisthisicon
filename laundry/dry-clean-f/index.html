Clothing labels

Dry clean (F)

Also called: hydrocarbon solvent, petroleum solvent, gentle dry clean

WTF it means

One for the professionals: dry cleaning with hydrocarbon (petroleum-based) solvents only. It's milder than the standard 'P' process.

What to do about it

Take it to a dry cleaner and point out the F. Not every cleaner uses hydrocarbon solvents.

For the record

F items often have sequins, beads, plastic trims or bonded fabrics that the stronger 'perc' solvent could damage.
