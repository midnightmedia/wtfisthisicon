Clothing labels

Line dry

Also called: hang to dry, clothesline, hang dry

WTF it means

Hang it up to dry on a clothesline, airer or hanger. No machine required.

What to do about it

Shake it out, hang it up and let it air-dry, indoors or out.

For the record

This curved-line version is common on US and older labels. The newer European (ISO) version has a single vertical line in the square instead.
