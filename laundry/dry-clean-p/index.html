Clothing labels

Dry clean (P)

Also called: professional dry clean, perchloroethylene, perc

WTF it means

Professional dry cleaning with tetrachloroethylene ('perc') or any of the milder hydrocarbon solvents is fine. This is the standard dry-cleaning process. Not, despite appearances, a parking sign.

What to do about it

Take it to a dry cleaner, who'll know exactly what P means. It's not something you can do at home.

For the record

A line under the circle asks the cleaner for a gentle process; two lines mean very gentle.
