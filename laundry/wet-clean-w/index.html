Clothing labels

Professional wet clean (W)

Also called: wet cleaning, professional wet cleaning

WTF it means

It can be professionally wet cleaned: a specialist water-based process with careful detergents and controlled drying. Confusingly, it's offered by many dry cleaners.

What to do about it

Take it to a cleaner that offers wet cleaning. It does not mean it can go in your machine at home (check the washtub symbol for that).

For the record

A crossed-out W means no professional wet cleaning.
