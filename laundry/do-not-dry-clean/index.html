Clothing labels

Do not dry clean

Also called: no dry cleaning

WTF it means

Don't dry clean it. The cleaning solvents would damage it.

What to do about it

Follow the washing symbols on the label instead (usually machine or hand wash).

For the record

You'll find it on items with rubber, plastic or coated parts that solvents would dissolve.
