Recycling labels

Plastic code 6 (PS)

Also called: PS, polystyrene, EPS, Styrofoam, resin identification code 6, RIC 6

WTF it means

Despite the arrows, this only tells you the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled. 6 = PS (polystyrene): foam takeaway boxes and packing blocks, disposable cups and cutlery, some yoghurt pots. Rarely recycled.

What to do about it

Usually general waste. Some recycling centres take clean packing foam, but check before you drive there.

For the record

Expanded polystyrene foam is around 98% air, which is a big part of why it's so uneconomical to collect and transport. Mostly, you'd be paying to move air.
