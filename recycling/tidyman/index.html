Recycling labels

Tidyman (don't litter)

Also called: Tidyman, Tidy Man, dispose of tidily, keep tidy, litter symbol

WTF it means

Please dispose of this tidily: in a bin, not on the street. It's an anti-litter reminder, not a recycling symbol.

What to do about it

Bin it. Whether that's the recycling or the general waste bin is a job for the other symbols on the pack: this little chap doesn't do sorting.

For the record

Popularised in the UK by the Keep Britain Tidy campaign.
