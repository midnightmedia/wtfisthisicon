Recycling labels

Don't recycle (UK on-pack label)

Also called: Don't Recycle, Do not recycle, not yet recycled, not currently recycled, OPRL, crossed-out recycling label

WTF it means

Fewer than half of UK councils collect this, or it can't yet be sorted and reprocessed properly. Put it in the recycling anyway and it can contaminate everything else in the load.

What to do about it

Put it in general waste, unless the pack sends you somewhere specific (a supermarket collection point or a take-back scheme). If your own council's list says it takes it, your council wins.

For the record

On a multi-part label, "Don't recycle" may only apply to one piece (often a film lid or a sleeve) while the rest says "Recycle". Separate them.
