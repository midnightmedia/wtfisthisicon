Recycling labels

PEFC (two trees in a circle)

Also called: PEFC, Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification, PEFC certified, PEFC recycled

WTF it means

The wood, paper or card comes from PEFC-certified, sustainably managed forests (or, on the "PEFC recycled" version, recycled material). Like FSC, it's a sourcing label, not a recycling instruction, despite the arrow round the trees.

What to do about it

Nothing to do because of this logo. Follow the recycling label or your council's rules to dispose of the packaging.

For the record

PEFC and FSC are rival schemes, so a pack normally shows one or the other, not both. Forestry has its own turf wars.
