Recycling labels

GL 70, 71, 72 (glass code)

Also called: GL, GL 70, GL 71, GL 72, glass code, clear glass, green glass, brown glass

WTF it means

European material code for glass: 70 = clear, 71 = green, 72 = brown. Glass bottles and jars can be recycled over and over without losing quality, which makes glass the overachiever of the recycling bin.

What to do about it

Empty bottles and jars go in your glass collection or a bottle bank; if the bank has separate slots, sort by colour. Glass is glass, except when it isn't: drinking glasses, Pyrex, window glass and light bulbs don't belong there.

For the record

Ovenproof and drinking glass melt at a different temperature from bottle glass, so even a little can spoil a whole batch.
