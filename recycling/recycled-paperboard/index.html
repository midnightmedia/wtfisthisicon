Recycling labels

100% Recycled Paperboard (US)

Also called: 100% Recycled Paperboard, RPA-100%, recycled paperboard symbol, recycled content

WTF it means

US mark meaning the box is made from 100% recycled paperboard (recovered paper fibre). It's about what the box is made of, not an instruction, though the box itself is usually recyclable.

What to do about it

Flatten it and put it in your paper and card recycling, as long as it's clean and dry.

For the record

In the US, the classic chasing arrows inside a solid circle have traditionally meant "made from recycled paper" as well, while plain arrows meant "recyclable". The whole difference is a filled-in circle.
