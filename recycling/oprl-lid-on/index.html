Recycling labels

Lid on / Cap on | Recycle (UK on-pack label)

Also called: Lid on, Cap on, Flatten, Flatten | Cap on, Insert straw, OPRL

WTF it means

Recycle it with the lid or cap put back on. Caps narrower than about 4 cm are too small for the sorting machines to catch on their own, but screwed onto the bottle they get recycled with it.

What to do about it

Empty it, put the lid back on and recycle. On drinks cartons you'll often see "Flatten | Cap on": squash it flat, then cap on.

For the record

On small juice cartons, "Insert straw" means push the straw back inside. If you remember being told to take lids off, you're not imagining it: that's older advice. Follow the label.
