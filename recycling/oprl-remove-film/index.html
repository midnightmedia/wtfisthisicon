Recycling labels

Multi-part label (Remove film / sleeve)

Also called: Remove film, Remove sleeve, multi-component label, Tray recycle, Film don't recycle, OPRL

WTF it means

The pack is made of bits that go different ways, so each part (tray, film, sleeve, box…) gets its own label. Typically the tray says "Recycle" and the film lid says "Don't recycle". "Remove film" or "Remove sleeve" means peel that bit off first.

What to do about it

Separate the pieces and follow each label: recyclable parts in the recycling, "Don't recycle" parts in general waste (or a supermarket collection point if the film's label says so).

For the record

A plastic sleeve left on a bottle can fool the sorting machines about what the bottle is made of. Look for a perforated strip or "peel here".
