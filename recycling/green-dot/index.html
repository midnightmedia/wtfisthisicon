Recycling labels

Green Dot

Also called: Der Grüne Punkt, Grüne Punkt, Green Point, Point Vert, Punto Verde

WTF it means

The manufacturer has paid into a packaging recovery scheme in some European countries. It does NOT mean the packaging is recyclable or made from recycled material, however much those arrows look the part.

What to do about it

Ignore it when sorting your rubbish. Follow your local rules or other recycling labels instead.

For the record

Started in Germany in 1991. It's so widely mistaken for a recycling symbol that some countries now actively discourage it. Usually printed in two shades of green, but it can be any colour.
