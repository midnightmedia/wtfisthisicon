Recycling labels

Metal Recycles Forever

Also called: Metal Recycles Forever, infinity loop, metal packaging recycling mark, Metal Packaging Europe

WTF it means

Industry mark (from Metal Packaging Europe, launched in 2014) for steel and aluminium packaging. It's a reminder that metal can be recycled again and again without losing quality, so please do.

What to do about it

Empty the can or tin and put it in your metal recycling.

For the record

You'll mostly see it on drinks cans and food tins sold in the UK and Europe. It says nothing about recycled content.
