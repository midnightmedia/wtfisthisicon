Recycling labels

PAP 20, 21, 22 (paper and card code)

Also called: PAP, PAP 20, PAP 21, PAP 22, paper code, cardboard code, material identification code

WTF it means

European material code for paper and card: 20 = corrugated cardboard (delivery boxes), 21 = other card (cereal boxes, sleeves), 22 = paper. Like the plastic numbers, it tells you what it's made of, not that it will definitely be recycled.

What to do about it

Clean, dry paper and card goes in your paper and card recycling. Greasy, food-stained, waxed or glittery bits often don't, and pizza boxes are the eternal grey area, so check your council's list.

For the record

These codes come from a 1997 EU decision and are voluntary. New EU-wide picture labels are due to start replacing them from late 2028.
