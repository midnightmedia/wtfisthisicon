Recycling labels

German deposit logo (DPG Pfand)

Also called: Pfand, Einwegpfand, DPG, Deutsche Pfandsystem, deposit return, bottle deposit

WTF it means

German deposit logo for single-use drinks bottles and cans (0.1 to 3 litres). A €0.25 deposit was added when the drink was sold in Germany, and you get it back when you return the empty. Free money, as long as you're in Germany.

What to do about it

In Germany, take it back empty and uncrushed (the machine has to read the label) to pretty much any shop that sells drinks in that kind of container. In the UK the deposit doesn't apply, sadly: recycle it as normal.

For the record

Reusable (Mehrweg) bottles carry a smaller deposit, usually 8 or 15 cents. Other countries have their own deposit logos, such as Ireland's Re-turn.
