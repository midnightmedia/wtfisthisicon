Recycling labels

Triman (France)

Also called: Triman, Info-tri, French recycling logo, French sorting logo

WTF it means

French marking that says a sorting rule applies to this item in France, so it shouldn't just go in general waste there. On its own it doesn't promise the item is recyclable. Since 2022 it comes with an "Info-tri" panel showing which French bin each part goes in.

What to do about it

In France, follow the Info-tri panel next to it. Anywhere else it doesn't apply: follow your own council's rules.

For the record

It's on loads of products sold across Europe because brands use one pack for several countries. The bin colours on the Info-tri (often yellow for packaging) won't necessarily match yours, so no need to go hunting for a yellow bin.
