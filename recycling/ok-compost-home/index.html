Recycling labels

OK compost HOME

Also called: OK compost HOME, home compostable, TÜV Austria, Vinçotte, OK compost

WTF it means

Certified by TÜV Austria to break down in an ordinary home compost heap, at the cooler, less steady temperatures of a garden bin, not just in an industrial plant.

What to do about it

It can go on your home compost heap (tear it up to help it along). Keep it out of your plastic recycling. Whether your food-waste caddy can take it is up to your council.

For the record

Home compostable still needs a compost heap's microbes, warmth and moisture: it's no excuse for littering.
