Recycling labels

OK compost INDUSTRIAL

Also called: OK compost INDUSTRIAL, industrially compostable, EN 13432, TÜV Austria, Vinçotte, OK compost

WTF it means

Certified to break down only in industrial composting plants, which run at about 55–60 °C (standard EN 13432). Your garden compost heap won't get anywhere near hot enough.

What to do about it

Not for your home compost heap or your plastic recycling. Use your food-waste caddy only if your council says it takes compostable packaging; otherwise general waste.

For the record

Same idea as the Seedling logo, from a different certifier. Industrial compostable is not home compostable: if it doesn't say HOME, assume your garden heap can't handle it.
