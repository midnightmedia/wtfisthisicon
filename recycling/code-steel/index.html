Recycling labels

FE 40 (steel code)

Also called: FE, FE 40, steel, tinplate, tin can, ferrous, steel code

WTF it means

European material code for steel (FE is from ferrum, Latin for iron, because why make it easy): food tins, pet-food tins, some aerosols and jar lids.

What to do about it

Empty and rinse it, then put it in your metal recycling. Steel tins are collected by virtually every UK council.

For the record

Steel is the easiest packaging to sort: at the sorting plant, big magnets simply pull it out of the mix.
