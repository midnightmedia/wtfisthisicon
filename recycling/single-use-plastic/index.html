Recycling labels

Plastic in product (turtle marking)

Also called: PLASTIC IN PRODUCT, PLASTIC IN FILTER, single-use plastics marking, SUP marking, turtle logo, dead turtle logo

WTF it means

EU marking for single-use products that contain plastic: wet wipes, sanitary pads, tampons and applicators, cigarette filters and disposable cups. The turtle isn't there to look cute: it's warning you there's plastic in it and that flushing or littering it harms wildlife.

What to do about it

Never flush wipes, pads or tampons: bag them and put them in general waste. Bin cups and cigarette ends rather than dropping them.

For the record

The left-hand picture and wording change by product: a crossed-out toilet on wipes and sanitary products, a crossed-out cup or cigarette on cups and filters. It's an EU rule, but you'll see it on plenty of products in UK shops.
