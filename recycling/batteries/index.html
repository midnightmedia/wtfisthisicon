Recycling labels

Crossed-out bin with Pb, Cd or Hg (batteries)

Also called: battery symbol, Pb, Cd, Hg, lead, cadmium, mercury, battery disposal, WEEE

WTF it means

Batteries must not go in your household rubbish. Letters under the bin warn that the battery contains heavy metals above set limits: Hg = mercury, Cd = cadmium, Pb = lead.

What to do about it

Take used batteries to a battery collection box (many supermarkets and shops that sell batteries have one) or your local recycling centre. Put tape over the ends of lithium and 9-volt batteries while you store them.

For the record

Batteries hidden in bins and recycling start fires in bin lorries and sorting plants. Some councils collect them from the kerb in a small bag left on top of your bin, so check yours.
