Recycling labels

FSC (tree with a tick)

Also called: FSC, Forest Stewardship Council, FSC Mix, FSC 100%, FSC Recycled, checkmark and tree

WTF it means

The wood, paper or card comes from FSC-certified, responsibly managed forests and/or recycled material. "FSC 100%" = all from FSC forests; "FSC Mix" = a blend of FSC, recycled and checked ("controlled") sources; "FSC Recycled" = recycled material. It's about where the card came from, not where it goes next.

What to do about it

Nothing to do because of this logo. For disposal, look for a recycling label; plain paper and card usually go in your paper and card recycling.

For the record

Every FSC label carries a licence code (FSC® C followed by numbers) that you can look up on the FSC website to see who's using it. Niche, but satisfying.
