Recycling labels

Crossed-out wheelie bin (WEEE)

Also called: WEEE, do not bin, e-waste symbol, waste electrical and electronic equipment

WTF it means

Don't put this in your household rubbish. Electrical items and batteries contain valuable and hazardous materials and must be collected separately.

What to do about it

Take it to a recycling centre or an electrical retailer's take-back scheme. Supermarkets and many shops have battery collection bins.

For the record

The little black bar under the bin isn't decoration: it means the product went on sale after 13 August 2005. Lithium batteries thrown in normal bins regularly start fires in bin lorries.
