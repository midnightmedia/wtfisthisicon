Recycling labels

Seedling (industrially compostable)

Also called: Seedling logo, compostable, industrially compostable, EN 13432, European Bioplastics, DIN CERTCO

WTF it means

Certified to break down in an industrial composting plant (European standard EN 13432), where it's kept at around 55–60 °C for weeks. Whatever the cheerful little plant suggests, it does NOT mean it will rot away in a garden compost heap, in a hedge or in the sea.

What to do about it

Never put it in your recycling bin: compostable plastic contaminates plastic recycling. Use your food-waste caddy only if your council says it takes compostable packaging or liners; otherwise it goes in general waste.

For the record

Some councils only accept food-caddy liners that show this logo, so it's worth looking for when you buy liners. "Biodegradable" on its own is a much vaguer claim.
