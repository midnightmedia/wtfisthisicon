Recycling labels

Recycle (UK on-pack label)

Also called: OPRL, On-Pack Recycling Label, Recycle label, widely recycled, check locally, Recycle Now swoosh, green recycling label

WTF it means

The UK's clearest "yes", which in recycling makes a nice change: at least three-quarters of UK councils collect this packaging (or this part of it) for recycling, and it can actually be sorted and reprocessed.

What to do about it

Put it in your household recycling and follow any extra word printed with it (Rinse, Lid on, Flatten…). If a pack has several labels, each one is for a different part.

For the record

Older packs say "Widely recycled" (same thing), "Check locally" (only some councils take it: look it up on your council's website or Recycle Now's Recycling Locator) or "Not yet recycled" (now "Don't recycle"). The labels are run by OPRL, a not-for-profit.
