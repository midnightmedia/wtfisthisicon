Recycling labels

Plastic code 5 (PP)

Also called: PP, polypropylene, resin identification code 5, RIC 5

WTF it means

Despite the arrows, this only tells you the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled. 5 = PP (polypropylene): margarine and yoghurt tubs, ready-meal trays, bottle caps and takeaway tubs.

What to do about it

Increasingly accepted in household recycling. Give it a rinse and check your council's list.

For the record

PP copes with heat, which is why it's popular for microwaveable containers.
