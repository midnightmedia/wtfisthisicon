Recycling symbols

Which bin? The arrows, decoded. 36 of them, decoded.

Recycling symbol (Mobius loop)

The item is technically capable of being recycled.

Recycling symbol with a percentage

The packaging contains that percentage of recycled material: 60% means 60% of it was made from recycled stuff.

Despite the arrows, this only tells you the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled.

Plastic code 6 (PS)

Despite the arrows, this only tells you the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled.

Plastic code 7 (Other)

Identifies the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled.

Green Dot

The manufacturer has paid into a packaging recovery scheme in some European countries.

Crossed-out wheelie bin (WEEE)

Don't put this in your household rubbish.

Tidyman (don't litter)

Please dispose of this tidily: in a bin, not on the street.

Recycle (UK on-pack label)

The UK's clearest "yes", which in recycling makes a nice change: at least three-quarters of UK councils collect this packaging (or this part of it) for recycling, and it can actually be sorted and reprocessed.

Don't recycle (UK on-pack label)

Fewer than half of UK councils collect this, or it can't yet be sorted and reprocessed properly.

Rinse | Recycle (UK on-pack label)

Recycle it, but give it a quick rinse first.

Lid on / Cap on | Recycle (UK on-pack label)

Recycle it with the lid or cap put back on.

Multi-part label (Remove film / sleeve)

The pack is made of bits that go different ways, so each part (tray, film, sleeve, box…) gets its own label.

Recycle with bags at large supermarkets

This soft plastic film (crisp packets, bread bags, fruit-and-veg bags, film lids, multipack wrap) can go to collection points at many larger supermarkets, usually near the entrance.

PAP 20, 21, 22 (paper and card code)

European material code for paper and card: 20 = corrugated cardboard (delivery boxes), 21 = other card (cereal boxes, sleeves), 22 = paper.

C/PAP 81–84 (drinks carton code)

Code for composite packaging, card bonded to other materials: 81 = card + plastic, 82 = card + aluminium, 83 = card + tinplate, 84 = card + plastic + aluminium (the classic juice, milk and soup carton).

GL 70, 71, 72 (glass code)

European material code for glass: 70 = clear, 71 = green, 72 = brown.

FE 40 (steel code)

European material code for steel (FE is from ferrum, Latin for iron, because why make it easy): food tins, pet-food tins, some aerosols and jar lids.

ALU 41 (aluminium code)

European material code for aluminium: drinks cans, foil, foil trays, some aerosols and tubes.

"alu" recycling mark

The item is made of aluminium, which is recyclable.

Metal Recycles Forever

Industry mark (from Metal Packaging Europe, launched in 2014) for steel and aluminium packaging.

Glass recycling figure (bottle into a bin)

Please recycle this glass: it belongs in a glass collection or bottle bank, not general waste.

Seedling (industrially compostable)

Certified to break down in an industrial composting plant (European standard EN 13432), where it's kept at around 55–60 °C for weeks.

OK compost HOME

Certified by TÜV Austria to break down in an ordinary home compost heap, at the cooler, less steady temperatures of a garden bin, not just in an industrial plant.

OK compost INDUSTRIAL

Certified to break down only in industrial composting plants, which run at about 55–60 °C (standard EN 13432).

FSC (tree with a tick)

The wood, paper or card comes from FSC-certified, responsibly managed forests and/or recycled material.

PEFC (two trees in a circle)

The wood, paper or card comes from PEFC-certified, sustainably managed forests (or, on the "PEFC recycled" version, recycled material).

Crossed-out bin with Pb, Cd or Hg (batteries)

Batteries must not go in your household rubbish.

Plastic in product (turtle marking)

EU marking for single-use products that contain plastic: wet wipes, sanitary pads, tampons and applicators, cigarette filters and disposable cups.

Triman (France)

French marking that says a sorting rule applies to this item in France, so it shouldn't just go in general waste there.

German deposit logo (DPG Pfand)

German deposit logo for single-use drinks bottles and cans (0.1 to 3 litres).

100% Recycled Paperboard (US)

US mark meaning the box is made from 100% recycled paperboard (recovered paper fibre).
