Recycling labels

Plastic code 4 (LDPE)

Also called: LDPE, PE-LD, low-density polyethylene, soft plastic, plastic film, resin identification code 4, RIC 4

WTF it means

Despite the arrows, this only tells you the plastic type, not whether it can be recycled. 4 = LDPE: carrier bags, bread bags, bubble wrap, squeezy bottles and plastic film.

What to do about it

Soft film often isn't collected from your doorstep, but many supermarkets have collection points for bags and wrappers. Check local rules.

For the record

Soft plastic can jam the sorting machines, which is why many councils still don't want it in the household recycling bin.
