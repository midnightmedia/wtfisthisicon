Freezer safe (snowflake)

Also called: freezer safe, suitable for freezing, freezable, snowflake symbol

WTF it means

Freezer safe: the container can go in the freezer without cracking. On food packaging, a snowflake means the food is suitable for home freezing.

What to do about it

Freeze away. Leave a little space for liquids to expand, and don't move glass straight from freezer to hot oven.

For the record

No snowflake on a container doesn't always mean it'll crack. But thin or brittle plastics often do in the cold.
