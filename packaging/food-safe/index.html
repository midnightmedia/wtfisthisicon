Packaging & labels

Food safe (glass and fork)

Also called: food contact material, food grade, suitable for food contact, glass and fork symbol

WTF it means

The material is safe to come into contact with food, so it's fine for storing, preparing or serving it. It's not advertising a wine bar.

What to do about it

Use it for food. It says nothing about the dishwasher, microwave or oven, so check for those symbols separately.

For the record

The same glass and fork crossed out is an unofficial but common way of saying "not for food".
