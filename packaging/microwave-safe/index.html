Microwave safe

Also called: microwavable, microwave safe, suitable for microwave

WTF it means

The container can go in the microwave without melting, warping or cracking. There's no single official design, but wavy lines are the giveaway.

What to do about it

Remove or loosen the lid (or pierce the film) before heating, and never microwave anything metallic. Some containers say "reheat only", which means they're not for long cooking.

For the record

"Microwave safe" means the container won't be damaged. It can still get very hot, so use oven gloves.
