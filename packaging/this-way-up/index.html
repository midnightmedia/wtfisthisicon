Packaging & labels

This way up

Also called: this side up, keep upright, orientation arrows, up arrows

WTF it means

This way up: the arrows point to the top of the box. Keep it upright, whatever the courier thinks.

What to do about it

Carry, store and stack the box with the arrows pointing up. It matters most for liquids, appliances and anything fragile inside.

For the record

Fridges and freezers often carry this mark because they should travel upright.
