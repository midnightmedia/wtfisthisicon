Packaging & labels

Flammable (flame in a red diamond)

Also called: GHS02, flammable, highly flammable, fire hazard, CLP flame

WTF it means

Flammable: the product, its vapour or its spray can catch fire easily. Common on aerosols, nail varnish remover, lighter fluid and some cleaning sprays.

What to do about it

Keep away from heat, sparks and naked flames. Don't spray near a hob, candle or cigarette. Store somewhere cool and don't puncture or burn aerosol cans.

For the record

These red-diamond pictograms replaced the old orange-square hazard symbols in the UK and EU around 2015–2017.
