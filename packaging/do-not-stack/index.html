Packaging & labels

Do not stack

Also called: do not stack, no stacking, stacking prohibited

WTF it means

Don't put anything on top of this box. The contents, or the box itself, can't take the weight.

What to do about it

Keep it on top of the pile, or give it a spot of its own.

For the record

A related symbol shows stacked boxes with a number or weight, which is the maximum you can stack on it.
