Packaging & labels

Dishwasher safe

Also called: dishwasher proof, dishwasher safe, top rack safe

WTF it means

Dishwasher safe: it can be washed in a dishwasher without damage. The symbol shows dishes being rained on, which is roughly what happens in there.

What to do about it

Put it in the dishwasher. If it says "top rack only", keep it away from the hotter bottom element. No symbol? Hand-wash to be safe, especially wood, cast iron, insulated mugs, sharp knives and anything with metallic trim.

For the record

Repeated dishwasher runs can still fade prints and cloud glassware over time. "Safe" means it won't break, not that it will look new forever.
