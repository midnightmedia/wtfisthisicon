UKCA mark

Also called: UKCA, UK Conformity Assessed, UKNI

WTF it means

UK Conformity Assessed: Great Britain's post-Brexit version of the CE mark. It's the maker declaring the product meets GB safety rules.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. You'll often see it alongside the CE mark, as many products can still use either in Great Britain. Belt and braces.

For the record

Products for Northern Ireland use the CE mark (sometimes with a UKNI mark) instead.
