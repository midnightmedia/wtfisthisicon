Packaging & labels

℮ (estimated sign)

Also called: e-mark, estimated sign, ℮ symbol, average quantity

WTF it means

The "estimated" sign means the pack was filled under the EU/UK average-quantity rules. The average across a batch must be at least the amount stated, and only a few packs may be slightly under, within strict limits. A small letter doing serious legal work.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. It's consumer protection, not a warning.

For the record

It's not "e for electric", energy or eco. It always sits right next to the weight or volume, like "500 g ℮".
