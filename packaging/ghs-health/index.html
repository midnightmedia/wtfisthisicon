Packaging & labels

Serious health hazard (person with a starburst)

Also called: GHS08, health hazard, CMR, aspiration hazard, starburst person

WTF it means

A serious or long-term health hazard: it may damage organs, cause cancer or harm fertility, cause breathing allergies, or be fatal if swallowed and it gets into the lungs. Common on lamp oil, white spirit and some fuels.

What to do about it

Follow the precautions exactly, use in a well-ventilated area and store it out of reach of children. If swallowed, do NOT make the person vomit. Get medical help immediately.

For the record

Lamp oil and white spirit get this symbol because a tiny sip can slip into a child's lungs.
