Packaging & labels

Period after opening (open jar)

Also called: PAO, period after opening, 12M, 6M, 24M, use within months, open jar symbol

WTF it means

How many months a cosmetic or toiletry stays good once you've opened it (12M means 12 months). That's why the jar on the symbol has its lid open.

What to do about it

Make a note when you open it and replace it after that time, or sooner if it smells, separates or changes texture. Be strict with eye products like mascara.

For the record

An hourglass symbol with a date means "best before". It's used for cosmetics that last less than 30 months even unopened.
