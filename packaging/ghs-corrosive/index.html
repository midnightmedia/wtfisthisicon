Corrosive (red diamond)

Also called: GHS05, corrosive, causes burns, corrosion symbol

WTF it means

Corrosive: can cause severe skin burns and eye damage, and may eat into metal. Found on oven cleaners, drain unblockers, strong descalers and some dishwasher products.

What to do about it

Wear gloves, avoid splashes and keep it away from children. If it gets on skin or in eyes, rinse with plenty of water for several minutes and get medical advice.

For the record

Never mix cleaning products: bleach mixed with acidic descalers or ammonia releases dangerous gases.
