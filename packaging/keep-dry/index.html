Packaging & labels

Keep dry (umbrella)

Also called: keep dry, protect from moisture, keep away from rain, umbrella symbol

WTF it means

Keep this parcel or product dry. Rain or damp can damage the contents.

What to do about it

Store it indoors, off damp floors, and don't leave it on the doorstep in the rain.

For the record

It's one of the international ISO 780 handling marks for parcels, alongside the wine glass (fragile) and the two arrows (this way up). No umbrella is supplied.
