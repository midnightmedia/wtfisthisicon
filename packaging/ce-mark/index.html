CE mark

Also called: CE, Conformité Européenne, European conformity, CE marking

WTF it means

The manufacturer declares that the product meets EU safety, health and environmental rules. For most products it's self-declared, not a quality mark or proof of independent testing.

What to do about it

Nothing to do. For electrical items and toys, a CE mark from a reputable seller is a basic sign it's been made for the EU market.

For the record

The story of a lookalike "China Export" mark is largely a myth, but badly copied or fake CE marks do exist, so the logo alone isn't a guarantee.
