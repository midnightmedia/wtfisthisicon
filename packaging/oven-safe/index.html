Oven safe

Also called: ovenproof, oven safe, suitable for oven

WTF it means

Oven safe: the dish or container can go in a conventional oven. There's no single official symbol. It's usually an oven shape, sometimes with a maximum temperature.

What to do about it

Check the maximum temperature, avoid the grill unless it says so, and don't put cold glass or ceramic straight into a very hot oven, as sudden temperature changes can crack it.

For the record

Oven safe doesn't automatically mean hob safe or microwave safe; those have their own symbols.
