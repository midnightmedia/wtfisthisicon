Air con & heat pumps

Swing / louvre direction

Also called: swing, SWING, vane, louvre, louver, flap, air direction, airflow direction

WTF it means

Controls the flap (louvre or vane) that steers the air. 'Swing' makes it sweep slowly up and down, or side to side, to spread the air round the room. Pressing again usually stops it where it is.

What to do about it

Press it to start the sweep and again to freeze the flap where you like it. For cooling, aim it slightly upwards (cold air sinks); for heating, point it down (warm air rises).

For the record

Brands call it Swing, Vane, Flap or Air Direction, as one name would have been too easy. Some remotes have two buttons, up/down and left/right. Don't push the flap by hand: its little motor gears break easily.
