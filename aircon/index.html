Cool mode

Cooling mode: the unit pulls heat out of the room, dumps it outside and blows cool, slightly drier air back in.

Heat mode

Heating mode: the unit works as a heat pump, pulling warmth out of the outdoor air (yes, even on cold days) and pumping it indoors.

Dry / dehumidify mode

Dry mode takes moisture out of the air rather than chasing a temperature.

Fan only mode

The indoor fan blows air round the room with no cooling or heating at all.

Auto mode

Automatic mode: the unit picks cooling, heating (or sometimes dry) by itself, by comparing the room temperature with the one you've set.

Swing / louvre direction

Controls the flap (louvre or vane) that steers the air.

Fan speed

Sets how hard the indoor fan blows.

Sleep / night mode

A bedtime setting: the unit runs quietly and slowly shifts the set temperature over the first hour or two (usually up by 1–2°C when cooling, or down when heating), so you don't wake up too cold or too hot.

Eco / energy saving

An energy-saving mode: the unit caps its maximum power, or nudges the set temperature a degree or two towards the outdoor temperature, so it uses less electricity.

Turbo / powerful / jet mode

Maximum blast: the fan and compressor run flat out for a short time to cool (or heat) the room as fast as possible.

Timer on / off

Sets the unit to switch itself on or off later, so the room is comfortable by the time you get in.

I Feel / Follow Me

The unit reads the temperature from a sensor inside the remote control, wherever you are, instead of its own sensor up near the ceiling.

Self-clean / iClean

A cleaning cycle for the inside of the indoor unit: it dries out the coil and fan (some models frost the coil over and then melt it to rinse off dust) to stop mould and musty smells.

Quiet mode

Runs the indoor fan (and on some models the outdoor unit too) at its slowest, quietest speed.
