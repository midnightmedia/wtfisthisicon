Air con & heat pumps

Auto mode

Also called: AUTO, automatic, auto changeover, heat/cool auto, smart mode

WTF it means

Automatic mode: the unit picks cooling, heating (or sometimes dry) by itself, by comparing the room temperature with the one you've set.

What to do about it

Set the temperature you want and let it decide. If it keeps flip-flopping between heating and cooling, or you only ever want one of them, pick cool or heat yourself.

For the record

Not to be confused with AUTO fan speed, usually shown next to the fan bars. Same word, same remote, different job: it only decides how hard the fan blows, not whether it heats or cools.
