Air con & heat pumps

Eco / energy saving

Also called: ECO, econo, econo cool, energy saver, economy, power saving

WTF it means

An energy-saving mode: the unit caps its maximum power, or nudges the set temperature a degree or two towards the outdoor temperature, so it uses less electricity.

What to do about it

Turn it on when the room is already comfortable and you just want to hold it there cheaply. Turn it off when you need the room cooled or heated quickly.

For the record

It works differently from brand to brand: some cap the compressor's power, some shift the temperature, and some also sweep the airflow so a warmer setting still feels cool. The leaf won't tell you which. The manual will.
