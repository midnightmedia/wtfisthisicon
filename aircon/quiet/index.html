Air con & heat pumps

Quiet mode

Also called: quiet, QUIET, silent, silence, whisper, low noise, outdoor unit quiet

WTF it means

Runs the indoor fan (and on some models the outdoor unit too) at its slowest, quietest speed. The trade-off: it cools or heats more slowly.

What to do about it

Use it at night, on calls, or when the room is already at temperature. Switch it off if the room isn't keeping up.

For the record

Some brands show a fan with a tiny bar, a moon, or just the word QUIET or SILENT. On some remotes it isn't its own button at all, just the lowest step of the fan speed button.
