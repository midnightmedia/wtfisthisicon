Air con & heat pumps

Turbo / powerful / jet mode

Also called: turbo, TURBO, powerful, jet, jet cool, super, hi power, fast cool, boost

WTF it means

Maximum blast: the fan and compressor run flat out for a short time to cool (or heat) the room as fast as possible.

What to do about it

Use it when you walk into a hot or freezing room. Most units drop back to normal by themselves after about 15–30 minutes, or once the room is at temperature. It's louder and uses more power while it's on.

For the record

Every brand names this differently (Turbo, Powerful, Jet, Super, Hi Power, Fast), and the symbol varies too: arrows, a rocket, a fan with speed lines, or just the word. Understatement isn't its strong point.
