Air con & heat pumps

Fan speed

Also called: fan speed, FAN, low / medium / high, auto fan, airflow, blower speed

WTF it means

Sets how hard the indoor fan blows. The bars show the level: one bar is low, two medium, three (or more) high. 'AUTO' or an 'A' by the fan lets the unit make up its own mind.

What to do about it

Use high (or auto) to reach the temperature quickly, then drop to low for quieter running. Auto is a good everyday choice.

For the record

Some remotes show the speed as wind lines or growing blades instead of bars, and many squeeze 'quiet' in below low and 'turbo' above high on the same button.
