Fridge & freezer

One star (*) freezer compartment

Also called: 1 star, one star, *, ice box, frozen food compartment, -6°C, star rating

WTF it means

A one-star compartment (usually the little ice box inside a fridge) only gets down to about −6°C. That's cold enough to keep food that's already frozen for about a week, but it isn't a real freezer. Think paddling pool, not swimming pool.

What to do about it

Use it for ice cubes and for shop-bought frozen food you'll eat within a week or so. Don't try to freeze fresh food in it.

For the record

Frozen food packets often carry a little star chart: roughly one star = 1 week, two stars = 1 month, three stars or a freezer = until the best-before date. Match the stars on the packet to the stars on your compartment.
