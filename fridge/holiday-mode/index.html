Fridge & freezer

Holiday / vacation mode

Also called: holiday, vacation, away mode, holiday mode, vacation mode, eco friendly

WTF it means

Saves energy while you're away. The freezer carries on as normal, but the fridge section is allowed to warm up: on Bosch and Samsung models, for example, it's held at about 14–15°C, far too warm for food.

What to do about it

Empty the fridge part before switching it on (the freezer can stay full), and switch it off when you're back. Let the fridge cool down again before you restock it.

For the record

Not every brand packs for the same holiday. LG's 'Vacation' (once called 'Eco Friendly') just nudges the fridge up to about 7°C and the freezer to −15°C. That's still too warm for milk and meat, so check your manual before leaving food in.
