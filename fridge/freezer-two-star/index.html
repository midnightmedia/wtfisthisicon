Fridge & freezer

Two star (**) freezer compartment

Also called: 2 star, two star, **, ice box, frozen food compartment, -12°C, star rating

WTF it means

A two-star compartment or section runs at about −12°C or colder. That keeps food that's already frozen for up to about a month, but it can't freeze fresh food properly.

What to do about it

Use it for ice and for frozen food you'll get through within a few weeks. For anything longer, or for freezing your own food, you need a four-star freezer.

For the record

Some proper freezers have a 'two-star section' too: a shelf or tray marked with two stars that runs warmer than the rest. It's the place for ice cream or things you'll eat soon, not the long-term stuff.
