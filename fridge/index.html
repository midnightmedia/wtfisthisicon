Fridge and freezer symbols

Stars, snowflakes & beeping doors. 24 of them, decoded.

High temperature alarm / red light

The freezer (or fridge) is warmer than it should be.

Sort it soon

Door open alarm

A door has been open (or not quite shut) for too long, usually a minute or two.

Sort it soon

Water filter indicator / filter reset

A reminder that the water filter for the dispenser and ice maker needs replacing.

Sort it soon

One star (*) freezer compartment

A one-star compartment (usually the little ice box inside a fridge) only gets down to about −6°C.

Two star (**) freezer compartment

A two-star compartment or section runs at about −12°C or colder.

Three star (***) freezer compartment

Runs at −18°C or colder, so it keeps food that's already frozen for about 3 months (often longer: check the packet).

Four star freezer (big star + three small stars)

The proper freezer rating.

Super freeze / fast freeze

Runs the freezer flat out, colder than normal, so a big batch of fresh food freezes right through quickly and the food already in there stays frozen.

Super cool / fast cool

Runs the fridge section at its coldest for a few hours to chill a big shop or warm drinks quickly, then calms back down to your normal temperature by itself.

Holiday / vacation mode

Saves energy while you're away.

Eco / energy-saving mode

Lets the fridge and freezer run a little warmer to use less electricity.

Child lock / control lock

The buttons are locked so nobody can change the temperature (or, on American-style fridges, use the water and ice dispenser) by accident.

Fan switch inside the fridge

Some fridges (Hotpoint, Indesit and others) have a fan inside the fridge section with its own on/off button.

Alarm off / mute button

Silences the beeping from a door or temperature alarm.

Water (dispenser)

Sets the dispenser on the front of the door to give chilled, filtered water when you press the paddle or button.

Cubed ice

Sets the dispenser to drop whole ice cubes.

Crushed ice

The dispenser runs the ice through a crusher before it drops into your glass, so you get small chips of ice instead of whole cubes.

Ice maker off

The automatic ice maker is switched off and won't make any more ice.

Humidity slider (salad drawer)

Opens or closes a vent on the salad (crisper) drawer.

0°C fresh / chill zone

A drawer kept colder than the rest of the fridge, just above freezing, for raw meat, fish and other food that goes off quickly.

'OK' temperature indicator

A little temperature gauge in the coldest part of some fridges (AEG, Smeg, Miele, Candy and others).

No Frost / Frost Free

A fan blows cold, dry air around so ice doesn't build up on the walls, and the appliance defrosts its own cooling coil automatically now and then.

Sabbath mode

A mode for observant Jewish households.

Climate class (SN, N, ST, T)

Printed on the rating plate (the sticker inside the fridge or on the back), it says what room temperatures the appliance is built to cope with: SN 10–32°C, N 16–32°C, ST 16–38°C, T 16–43°C.
