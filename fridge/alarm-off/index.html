Fridge & freezer

Alarm off / mute button

Also called: alarm off, alarm, mute, silence, warning signal off, alarm reset, buzzer off

WTF it means

Silences the beeping from a door or temperature alarm. It stops the noise, not the problem: the display may keep flashing until the door is shut or the freezer is back down to temperature.

What to do about it

Press it once to stop the beep, then work out why it went off (door open? freezer too warm?). If the alarm keeps coming back, whatever caused it hasn't gone away.

For the record

On some brands the same button doubles as the button lock when held for a few seconds (Neff labels it 'alarm off/lock'), so a long press can lock the panel by accident. Two problems for the price of one.
