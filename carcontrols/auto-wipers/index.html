Car buttons

Automatic wipers (AUTO on the wiper stalk)

Also called: rain sensing wipers, auto wipers, rain sensor, AUTO wipe

WTF it means

In this position the wipers run themselves: a rain sensor behind the windscreen (by the mirror) speeds them up or slows them down as the rain changes.

What to do about it

Move the wiper stalk to AUTO (often the first click). The ring or wheel on the stalk sets how sensitive they are. Switch it off before a car wash, or the wipers may start halfway through.
