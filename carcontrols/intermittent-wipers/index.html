Car buttons

Intermittent wipers (wiper with a dashed line)

Also called: INT, intermittent wipe, wiper delay, occasional wipe

WTF it means

The wipers make one sweep, pause, then sweep again, for drizzle that isn't worth the full-time wipers. The dashed line is the pause.

What to do about it

Select INT (or the dashed-line symbol) on the wiper stalk, and use the ring or wheel on the stalk to set the gap between wipes. Pulling the stalk towards you usually squirts washer fluid.
