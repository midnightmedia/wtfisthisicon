Car buttons

SOS button (eCall)

Also called: eCall, emergency call, SOS call button, roof SOS button

WTF it means

The emergency call button, usually in the roof panel above the mirror. It calls the emergency services and sends them the car's location. The car also calls by itself after a serious crash, for example if the airbags go off.

What to do about it

Only press it in a real emergency on the road. Pressed it by mistake? Don't hang up: stay on the line and tell the operator it was an accident.

For the record

It's been required on every new car model sold in the EU since 2018. A button next to it with a spanner or an i is usually for breakdown help or the car maker's assistance line instead.
