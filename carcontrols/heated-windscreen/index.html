Car buttons

Heated windscreen (windscreen with wavy lines)

Also called: heated front screen, Quickclear, front windscreen heater, heated windshield

WTF it means

Switches on the fine heating wires built into the windscreen, which clear frost and fog in a minute or two, much faster than blowing hot air at it. Ford calls it Quickclear.

What to do about it

Press it on frosty mornings; it switches itself off after a few minutes. If you can see faint wiggly lines in your windscreen, you've got one. Wavy arrows instead of plain wavy lines is the ordinary demister, which uses the fan.
