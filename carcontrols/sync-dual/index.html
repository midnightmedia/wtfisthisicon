Car buttons

SYNC and DUAL buttons (climate control)

Also called: SYNC, DUAL, dual zone climate, climate sync, temperature sync

WTF it means

Both are about dual-zone climate control, where the driver and front passenger each get their own temperature. SYNC (or switching DUAL off) makes the passenger side copy the driver's settings. DUAL lets each side set their own.

What to do about it

Press SYNC when you want one temperature for the whole car, set from the driver's side. Turn the passenger's knob (or press DUAL) to split them again, so whoever is always cold can be cold on their own side.
