Car buttons

Hazard warning lights

Also called: hazards, flashers, emergency lights

WTF it means

Flashes all the indicators at once to tell other drivers you've stopped or there's a hazard ahead.

What to do about it

Use them when you've broken down, when you're stopped somewhere unsafe, or (in some countries) to warn of a sudden queue on a motorway. They're not a 'park anywhere' card, however often they get used as one.
