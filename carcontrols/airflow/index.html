Car buttons

Air flow direction

Also called: vent mode, air distribution, face and feet vents

WTF it means

Chooses where the air comes out. The arrows point at the vents in use: face, feet or both. A version with a windscreen line sends air to the windscreen too. The little seated person is you, looking admirably relaxed.

What to do about it

Feet in winter (heat rises), face in summer, windscreen + feet when the windows fog up.
