Car button and switch symbols

Climate, lights & the mystery switches. 22 of them, decoded.

Air recirculation

The ventilation recycles the air already inside the car instead of pulling in fresh air from outside.

Fresh air intake

The ventilation is pulling fresh air in from outside (the opposite of recirculation).

Windscreen demister

Blasts warm, dry air at the inside of the windscreen to clear fog or frost.

Rear window heater

Switches on the thin heating wires in the rear window to melt frost and clear fog.

Air conditioning (snowflake button)

On the climate-control panel, the snowflake turns the air conditioning compressor on or off.

Air flow direction

Chooses where the air comes out.

Heated seat

Turns on the seat heater, the best button in the car on a cold morning.

Heated steering wheel

Turns on the heater built into the rim of the steering wheel.

Hazard warning lights

Flashes all the indicators at once to tell other drivers you've stopped or there's a hazard ahead.

Automatic wipers (AUTO on the wiper stalk)

In this position the wipers run themselves: a rain sensor behind the windscreen (by the mirror) speeds them up or slows them down as the rain changes.

Intermittent wipers (wiper with a dashed line)

The wipers make one sweep, pause, then sweep again, for drizzle that isn't worth the full-time wipers.

Rear wiper

Controls the wiper on the rear window.

Automatic headlights (AUTO on the light switch)

In this position the headlights switch themselves on when it gets dark (or you drive into a tunnel), using a light sensor at the top of the windscreen.

Headlight levelling dial (0 to 3)

This dial lowers the headlight beams when the back of the car is weighed down, so you don't dazzle oncoming drivers.

Window lock (crossed-out window)

Locks the passengers' window switches (on most cars, every one except the driver's), so children can't open the windows, or play with them for the whole journey.

Mirror fold button

Folds the door mirrors in against the windows, and back out again.

SYNC and DUAL buttons (climate control)

Both are about dual-zone climate control, where the driver and front passenger each get their own temperature.

Parking sensors button (P with waves)

Switches the parking sensors on or off: the ones that beep faster and faster as you get closer to something.

Seat memory buttons (M, 1, 2, 3)

These save your driving position.

Ventilated seat (seat with arrows)

Turns on small fans in the seat that move air through the perforated upholstery, so you don't stick to it on hot days.

SOS button (eCall)

The emergency call button, usually in the roof panel above the mirror.

Heated windscreen (windscreen with wavy lines)

Switches on the fine heating wires built into the windscreen, which clear frost and fog in a minute or two, much faster than blowing hot air at it.
