Car button and switch symbols

Climate, lights & the mystery switches. 9 of them, decoded.

Air recirculation

The ventilation recycles the air already inside the car instead of pulling in fresh air from outside.

Fresh air intake

The ventilation is pulling fresh air in from outside (the opposite of recirculation).

Windscreen demister

Blasts warm, dry air at the inside of the windscreen to clear fog or frost.

Rear window heater

Switches on the thin heating wires in the rear window to melt frost and clear fog.

Air conditioning (snowflake button)

On the climate-control panel, the snowflake turns the air conditioning compressor on or off.

Air flow direction

Chooses where the air comes out.

Heated seat

Turns on the seat heater, the best button in the car on a cold morning.

Heated steering wheel

Turns on the heater built into the rim of the steering wheel.

Hazard warning lights

Flashes all the indicators at once to tell other drivers you've stopped or there's a hazard ahead.
