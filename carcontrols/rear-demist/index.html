Car buttons

Rear window heater

Also called: rear defrost, rear demister, heated rear window, rear defogger

WTF it means

Switches on the thin heating wires in the rear window to melt frost and clear fog. It often heats the door mirrors too. So that's what those faint lines are for.

What to do about it

Press to switch it on. Most cars turn it off automatically after 10–15 minutes.
