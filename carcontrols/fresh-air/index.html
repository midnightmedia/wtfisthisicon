Car buttons

Fresh air intake

Also called: outside air, air intake, fresh air mode

WTF it means

The ventilation is pulling fresh air in from outside (the opposite of recirculation). The giveaway is the arrow coming in from outside the car.

What to do about it

Use this most of the time: it keeps the windows clear and the air fresh.
