Car buttons

Air recirculation

Also called: recirc, recycled air, inside air

WTF it means

The ventilation recycles the air already inside the car instead of pulling in fresh air from outside. The arrow going round in circles is, for once, exactly what it looks like.

What to do about it

Great for keeping out smells and fumes (tunnels, traffic, that farm) or cooling down fast with the air-con. Don't leave it on for long: the windows fog up and the air gets stuffy (and CO₂ builds up, making you drowsy).
