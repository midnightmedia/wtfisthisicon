Car buttons

Ventilated seat (seat with arrows)

Also called: cooled seat, seat ventilation, air-conditioned seat, seat cooling, climate seat

WTF it means

Turns on small fans in the seat that move air through the perforated upholstery, so you don't stick to it on hot days. Pressing it repeatedly usually cycles through speeds.

What to do about it

Use it in summer, but don't expect air-con levels of cold: it's gentle. Wavy lines instead of straight arrows is the heated seat button, which is a sweaty surprise in July.
