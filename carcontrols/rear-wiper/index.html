Car buttons

Rear wiper

Also called: rear wiper, rear washer, back window wiper, tailgate wiper

WTF it means

Controls the wiper on the rear window. A version with a little fountain squirts washer fluid on it too.

What to do about it

On most cars, push the wiper stalk away from you (or twist its end) to switch the rear wiper on, and push or twist further to wash. Many cars also wipe the back window automatically when you select reverse with the front wipers on.
