Car buttons

Heated seat

Also called: seat heater, seat warmer

WTF it means

Turns on the seat heater, the best button in the car on a cold morning. Pressing it repeatedly usually cycles through heat levels (shown by little lights).

What to do about it

Enjoy. A version with a fan or blue arrows is seat ventilation or cooling instead, which is a chilly surprise in winter.
