Car buttons

Window lock (crossed-out window)

Also called: window lock, child lock, rear window disable, child safety lock, window switch lock

WTF it means

Locks the passengers' window switches (on most cars, every one except the driver's), so children can't open the windows, or play with them for the whole journey. The driver's own switches still work.

What to do about it

Press it on the driver's door switch panel; a light on the button shows it's on. On some cars the same button also locks the rear doors from the inside (an electric child lock).
