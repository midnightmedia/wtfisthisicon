Car buttons

Parking sensors button (P with waves)

Also called: park assist, parking aid, park distance control, PDC, parking sensor switch, reversing sensors

WTF it means

Switches the parking sensors on or off: the ones that beep faster and faster as you get closer to something. Most cars switch them on automatically in reverse; this button turns them on for going forwards too, or quietens them.

What to do about it

Press it to switch the sensors on in tight spots, or off when they're beeping at a hedge, a tow bar or a bike rack. Keep the little round sensors in the bumpers clean, as mud and snow confuse them.
