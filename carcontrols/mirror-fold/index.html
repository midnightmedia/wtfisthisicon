Car buttons

Mirror fold button

Also called: fold mirrors, power folding mirrors, mirror retract, mirror fold switch

WTF it means

Folds the door mirrors in against the windows, and back out again. Useful in tight car parks, narrow streets and car washes.

What to do about it

Press the button (or turn the mirror knob to the fold position). Many cars also fold them automatically when you lock up. If a mirror has been knocked out of place, folding them in and out electrically often clicks it back into position.
