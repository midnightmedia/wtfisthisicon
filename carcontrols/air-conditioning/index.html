Car buttons

Air conditioning (snowflake button)

Also called: A/C, AC, aircon, air con

WTF it means

On the climate-control panel, the snowflake turns the air conditioning compressor on or off.

What to do about it

Use it to cool the car, and in winter too. It sounds backwards, but A/C dries the air, so it clears foggy windows much faster.

For the record

The same snowflake glowing amber on the dashboard is the frost/ice warning instead.
