Tumble dryer

Clean lint filter

Also called: clean fluff filter, lint screen, filter light, clean filter

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The fluff (lint) filter is full or needs a clean. A clogged filter makes drying slow and expensive, and lint build-up is a fire risk.

What to do about it

Pull out the filter (usually in the door opening), peel or brush off the fluff and slide it back in. Ideally, do this after every load.

For the record

If it looks clean but the light stays on, rinse the filter under the tap and let it dry. Fabric softener can leave an invisible film that blocks the mesh.
