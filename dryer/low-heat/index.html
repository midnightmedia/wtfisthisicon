Tumble dryer

Low heat / delicate

Also called: low temperature, gentle, delicates, low heat

WTF it means

Dries at a lower temperature for delicate, synthetic or heat-sensitive items. Bosch marks it with a feather; other makers use a thermometer or simply print the word.

What to do about it

Use it for anything whose label shows the tumble-dry symbol with one dot. It takes longer, but it saves your clothes.

For the record

Some dryers use the care-label symbol itself here: a circle in a square with one dot.
