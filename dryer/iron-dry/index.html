Tumble dryer

Iron dry

Also called: damp dry, ready to iron

WTF it means

Leaves the laundry slightly damp, which makes ironing much easier. The dryer still won't do the ironing for you, sadly.

What to do about it

Use it for shirts, linen and cotton you plan to iron, and iron them soon after the cycle.

For the record

Slightly damp fabric irons faster and smoother than bone-dry fabric, so this saves effort, not just energy.
