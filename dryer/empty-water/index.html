Tumble dryer

Empty water container

Also called: water tank full, empty tank, condensate container, drain tank

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The water tank is full. Condenser and heat-pump dryers collect the water from your clothes in a tank, and the dryer will stop (or won't start) until you empty it.

What to do about it

Pull out the water container (often a drawer at the top, or behind the bottom flap), pour it down the sink and push it fully back in.

For the record

If the dryer stands near a drain or sink, most models can pipe the water away through a hose, and then you'll never have to empty the tank again.
