Tumble dryer

Clean condenser / heat-pump filter

Also called: clean condenser, clean heat exchanger, base filter, clean heat pump filter

Sort it soon

WTF it means

The condenser, or the extra filter at the bottom of a condenser or heat-pump dryer, is clogged with fluff and needs cleaning.

What to do about it

Open the flap at the bottom front and take out the condenser or base filter. Rinse off the fluff under the tap, let it drip dry and refit it. Your manual shows how it comes apart.

For the record

A blocked condenser is the most common reason a heat-pump dryer suddenly takes hours to dry a load.
