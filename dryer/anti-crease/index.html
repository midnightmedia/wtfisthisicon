Tumble dryer

Anti-crease

Also called: wrinkle guard, wrinkle prevent, crease guard, anti-wrinkle, extended tumble

WTF it means

After drying, the drum keeps turning briefly every few minutes with cool air, so clothes don't settle into creases while they wait for you.

What to do about it

Turn it on if you won't be there when the cycle ends. Opening the door stops it.

For the record

The display may say 'End' or keep blinking while it's in anti-crease mode. Whatever the blinking suggests, it's finished and you can open it any time.
