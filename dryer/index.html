Tumble dryer symbols

Why is it beeping? 9 of them, decoded.

Clean lint filter

The fluff (lint) filter is full or needs a clean.

Sort it soon

Empty water container

The water tank is full.

Sort it soon

Clean condenser / heat-pump filter

The condenser, or the extra filter at the bottom of a condenser or heat-pump dryer, is clogged with fluff and needs cleaning.

Sort it soon

Anti-crease

After drying, the drum keeps turning briefly every few minutes with cool air, so clothes don't settle into creases while they wait for you.

Cupboard dry

Dries clothes completely, ready to fold and put straight into the cupboard.

Iron dry

Leaves the laundry slightly damp, which makes ironing much easier.

Extra dry (cupboard dry +)

Dries even more thoroughly than cupboard dry, for thick items like towels, jeans or bedding that stay stubbornly damp in the seams.

Low heat / delicate

Dries at a lower temperature for delicate, synthetic or heat-sensitive items.

Timed dry

Dries for a fixed time you choose, instead of stopping when the sensor decides the clothes are dry.
