Air fryers

Bacon (bacon preset)

Also called: bacon, rashers, streaky bacon, bacon preset, BACON

WTF it means

The bacon preset, drawn as a wavy rasher of streaky.

What to do about it

On a Cosori it starts at 160°C (320°F) for 8 minutes. Lay the rashers flat in one layer; thick back bacon may want a couple of minutes more.

For the record

Fat drips into the bottom of the basket. Cosori says to pour it away after cooking, or it smokes next time you use it.
