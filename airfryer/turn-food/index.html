Air fryers

Turn Food on the display

Also called: Turn Food, TURN FOOD, turn food message, flip food, turn reminder

WTF it means

A reminder, part-way through cooking, to turn the food over. It's Instant Vortex's version of the shake reminder.

What to do about it

Pull the basket out, flip or turn the food, and slide it back in; cooking carries on. Do nothing and it carries on anyway after 10 seconds.

For the record

Flat things like burgers, chicken breasts and fish fillets want turning; small things like chips just want a shake.
