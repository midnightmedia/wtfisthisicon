What normal people call it

endend in digits3nd

Air fryers

End on the display

Also called: End, END, End message, cooking finished, program ended

WTF it means

Cooking's finished. Cosori and Instant Vortex air fryers both show End when the time runs out, along with some beeping.

What to do about it

Take the food out, or add a few more minutes if it isn't done. An Instant beeps once a minute for up to 10 minutes until you deal with it.

For the record

Food left in a hot drawer keeps cooking for a while, so get chips out promptly if you like them crisp rather than crunchy.
