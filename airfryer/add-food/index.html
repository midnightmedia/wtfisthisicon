Air fryers

Add Food on the display

Also called: Add Food, ADD FOOD, add food message, preheated, preheat done

WTF it means

The air fryer has finished preheating and is ready for your food. Instant Vortex models show it on Air Fry, Roast, Bake and Grill (Broil on American models).

What to do about it

Pull out the basket, put the food in and slide it back. Cooking and the countdown start once the basket is back in.

For the record

Cosori does the same job with READY and a row of dashes after preheating. Different words, same message: hurry up with the chips.
