Air fryers

MATCH button (dual zone)

Also called: MATCH, match, SyncCook, sync cook, match cook, Ninja match, dual zone match

WTF it means

MATCH copies the function, temperature and time from drawer 1 to drawer 2, for a double batch of the same thing. Instant calls its version SyncCook.

What to do about it

Set drawer 1, press MATCH, then START/STOP. Both drawers then cook with the same settings, side by side.

For the record

On an Instant, changing the time or temperature keeps SyncCook on for both zones, but pick one zone to change and SyncCook switches off.
