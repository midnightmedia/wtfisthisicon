Air fryers

Carrot (vegetables preset)

Also called: vegetables, veg, veggies, vegetable preset, VEG

WTF it means

The vegetables preset. Cosori draws a carrot but means any veg: broccoli, peppers, courgettes, sprouts.

What to do about it

On a Cosori it starts at 160°C (320°F) for 8 minutes with the shake reminder on. Cut everything to a similar size, toss it in a little oil, and shake when it reminds you.

For the record

Potatoes and other root veg take a lot longer than 8 minutes. Some Cosori models have a separate root vegetables preset for exactly that.
