The machine wants you to run its descaling programme.

Flashing lights while warming up

Nearly every coffee machine blinks while it heats up and goes steady when it's ready.

Relax, it's fine

Rinse

Runs hot water through the spouts to rinse and warm the coffee circuit.

Espresso / short coffee (small cup)

Makes a small, strong coffee.

Lungo / long coffee (large cup)

Makes a longer coffee, with more water through the coffee.

Two cups / double

Makes two coffees at once, one under each spout.

Hot water

Dispenses plain hot water (for tea, an Americano or warming cups) from a separate hot-water spout or the steam nozzle.

Steam (milk frothing)

Turns on steam for frothing milk through the steam wand (also called the cappuccino maker or frother).

Cappuccino / latte (milk drinks)

One-touch milk drinks on machines with a milk carafe or jug (Philips LatteGo, De'Longhi LatteCrema, Jura, Melitta and others).

Coffee strength / aroma (beans)

Sets how strong the coffee is by changing how much is ground per cup: one bean (mild) to three beans (strong) on Melitta, three steps next to a bean on Philips, one or two beans on Jura, or a dial from MIN to MAX round two beans on De'Longhi's Magnifica S.

Pre-ground coffee (scoop)

Tells the machine to use ground coffee you've tipped into the separate chute instead of grinding beans.

Thermometer (heating up / temperature)

It means different things on different machines, which is the least helpful thing a symbol can do.

Pressure gauge (Sage espresso machines)

Shows the pressure of the water going through the coffee while a shot pours, on the Sage Barista Express (sold as Breville in many countries) and similar machines.

ECO / energy saving

Energy-saving mode is on: the machine doesn't keep itself fully hot between drinks.
